Watching British TV in the US

How can I watch British TV in the USA?

The three essential services are BritBox (the largest library of BBC and ITV classics), Acorn TV (the mystery specialist), and PBS Passport (home of Masterpiece). Free options like Tubi and Pluto TV carry older classics with ads. For UK-only platforms like BBC iPlayer, you'll need a VPN. See the complete breakdown in our 2026 streaming guide.

What is the best British TV streaming service?

For most fans, BritBox is the best single subscription — no other service comes close to its depth of British classics and current series. If you love mysteries specifically, Acorn TV is a strong rival and many fans subscribe to both. Our streaming guide compares them side by side.

Is BritBox worth it for British TV fans?

Yes, for serious British TV fans, BritBox is essential. It carries the largest legal library of BBC and ITV content available in the US, including classics that simply aren't available anywhere else.

Is Acorn TV worth it?

If you love British mysteries, yes. Acorn TV specialises in British and international crime drama and is the only home of shows like Hinterland, Doc Martin, and Agatha Raisin. It pairs perfectly with BritBox.

Can I watch BBC iPlayer in the US with a VPN?

Yes — a VPN routes your connection through a UK server so BBC iPlayer sees a UK IP address and works normally. NordVPN is the most reliable option.

Where can I watch British TV shows for free in the US?

Tubi offers a large free library including older series of Inspector Morse and Midsomer Murders. Pluto TV has dedicated British TV channels. Kanopy is free with many public library cards.

What to Watch

What British TV show should I watch first?

In our opinion, the best starting points are Vera or Agatha Christie's Poirot. Or answer three quick questions in Match Me a Mystery for a personal pick.

What is the best British detective show of all time?

Most fans and critics point to Inspector Morse as the gold standard. For modern shows, Broadchurch and Happy Valley regularly top audience polls. Vera is the most consistently beloved long-runner.

What is the most underrated British mystery series?

Hinterland consistently tops lists of overlooked gems. Other underappreciated favourites include The Detectorists, Hope Street, and The Bletchley Circle. Download our free 25 Hidden Gems guide.

What are the best cosy British mysteries?

Start with Miss Marple (the 1984 Joan Hickson version), Agatha Raisin on Acorn TV, Midsomer Murders, and Father Brown. See our full guide to cozy British mysteries.
