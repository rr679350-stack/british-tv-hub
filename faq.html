Watching British TV in the US

How can I watch British TV in the USA?

The three essential services are BritBox (the largest library of BBC and ITV classics), Acorn TV (the mystery specialist), and PBS Passport (home of Masterpiece). Free options like Tubi and Pluto TV carry older classics with ads. For UK-only platforms like BBC iPlayer, you'll need a VPN. See the complete breakdown in our 2026 streaming guide.

What is the best British TV streaming service?

For most fans, BritBox is the best single subscription — no other service comes close to its depth of British classics and current series. If you love mysteries specifically, Acorn TV is a strong rival and many fans subscribe to both, since their libraries barely overlap. Our streaming guide compares them side by side.

Is BritBox worth it for British TV fans?

Yes, for serious British TV fans, BritBox is essential. It carries the largest legal library of BBC and ITV content available in the US, including classics that simply aren't available anywhere else. If you can only choose one British TV subscription, make it BritBox. See our full streaming guide.

Is Acorn TV worth it?

If you love British mysteries, yes. Acorn TV specialises in British and international crime drama, carries many exclusive US premieres, and is the only home of shows like Hinterland, Doc Martin, and Agatha Raisin. It pairs perfectly with BritBox — their libraries rarely overlap. Compare both in our streaming guide.

What is the difference between BritBox and Acorn TV?

BritBox focuses primarily on British content from the BBC and ITV, with a vast library of classic and contemporary series. Acorn TV is broader in scope, covering British and international mysteries, and tends to have more exclusive US premieres of newer shows. Many serious fans subscribe to both — they complement each other well. See the full comparison in our streaming guide.

Can I watch BBC shows for free in the United States?

The BBC iPlayer is only available to UK residents. In the US, BBC content reaches you through BritBox, PBS Passport, and select titles on Amazon Prime. Some older BBC content is also available free with ads on Tubi. There is no legitimate free access to current BBC programming from the US.

What to Watch

What British TV show should I watch first?

In our opinion, the best starting points are Vera — instantly engaging, with Brenda Blethyn's wonderful DCI Stanhope — or Agatha Christie's Poirot, the gold standard of classic British mystery. We've ranked the easiest entry points in our beginner's guide — or answer three quick questions in Match Me a Mystery for a personal pick.

What is the most popular British TV show?

Globally, Downton Abbey and The Crown are the most-watched British exports. But in our opinion, the shows that British mystery fans truly return to again and again are Inspector Morse, Vera, and Happy Valley. See the full list of fan favourites.

What is the best British detective show of all time?

Most fans and critics point to Inspector Morse as the gold standard — its combination of literary intelligence, superb acting from John Thaw, and Oxford atmosphere has never truly been matched. For modern shows, Broadchurch and Happy Valley regularly top audience polls. Vera is the most consistently beloved long-runner.

What is the most underrated British mystery series?

Hinterland consistently tops lists of overlooked gems — beautifully shot in rural Wales with a genuinely distinctive atmosphere. Other underappreciated favourites include The Detectorists, Hope Street, and The Bletchley Circle. For more, download our free 25 Hidden Gems guide.

What are the best cosy British mysteries?

Start with Miss Marple (the 1984 Joan Hickson version is definitive), Agatha Raisin on Acorn TV, Midsomer Murders, and Father Brown. The Detectorists isn't a mystery but has the same gentle, comforting quality that cosy mystery fans love. See our full guide to cozy British mysteries.

What should I watch if I loved Downton Abbey?

Try Upstairs Downstairs (the original that inspired it), The Crown, Cranford, Poldark, or Victoria. All share Downton's gorgeous production values, strong ensemble casts, and period drama sweep. All Creatures Great and Small is a gentler but equally warm choice.
