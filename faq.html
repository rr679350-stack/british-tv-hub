Watching British TV in the US

Is BritBox worth it for British TV fans?

Yes, for serious British TV fans, BritBox is essential. It carries the largest legal library of BBC and ITV content available in the US, including classics that simply aren't available anywhere else. If you can only choose one British TV subscription, make it BritBox. See our full streaming guide.

Can I watch BBC shows for free in the United States?

The BBC iPlayer is only available to UK residents. In the US, BBC content reaches you through BritBox, PBS Passport, and select titles on Amazon Prime. Some older BBC content is also available free with ads on Tubi. There is no legitimate free access to current BBC programming from the US.

What is the difference between BritBox and Acorn TV?

BritBox focuses primarily on British content from the BBC and ITV, with a vast library of classic and contemporary series. Acorn TV is broader in scope, covering British and international mysteries, and tends to have more exclusive US premieres of newer shows. Many serious fans subscribe to both — they complement each other well. See the full comparison in our streaming guide.

Where can I watch British TV shows for free in the US?

Tubi offers a surprisingly large free library supported by ads, including older series of Inspector Morse and Midsomer Murders. Pluto TV has dedicated British TV channels running around the clock. Kanopy is free with many public library cards. Both BritBox and Acorn TV offer free trials. Full details in our free options guide.
