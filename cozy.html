What makes a British mystery "cozy"?

A cozy mystery is warm where other crime dramas are cold. The detective is charming rather than tortured, the setting is picturesque rather than bleak, and while there's always a murder, it never feels threatening or disturbing. Think village fetes, eccentric suspects, and a satisfying resolution over a cup of tea. A great cozy mystery has:

  • A recurring detective you genuinely like spending time with
  • A beautiful, distinctive setting — usually rural English countryside
  • Clever plotting without graphic violence or dark themes
  • Warmth, wit, and often a touch of gentle humour
  • The satisfying feeling of order restored by the end of each episode
