From sun-soaked island mysteries to a long-weekend prestige binge, here are 25 shows to keep the kettle on for — sorted by mood, with where to stream every one in the US.

A Little of Everything

Thanksgiving week means different things depending on the day — a quiet afternoon with tea and something gentle, or a long weekend with time to properly binge something more intense. This list is sorted by mood, from the coziest village mysteries to a handful of prestige dramas worth saving for when the house finally goes quiet.
