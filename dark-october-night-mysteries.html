14 British Mysteries for a Dark October Night

The nights are getting longer, the kettle's on, and somewhere in a quiet English village, something is definitely not quite right. A countdown from cosy cases with a hint of autumn unease to proper lights-on-territory viewing.

Start the Countdown →
Jump to Start Cosy (14–11) Getting Atmospheric (10–7) Turning Eerie (6–4) Lights On (3–1) Where to Watch

Curated and verified by British TV Hub · Streaming availability checked September 2026

For October, we've gathered 14 British mysteries made for dark evenings — a mix of standout episodes, specials, and series perfect for seasonal viewing. Start at No. 14 if you want cosy. Keep going if you dare.

Start Cosy

Comfort viewing with just enough autumn chill to feel seasonal.

14
Father Brown
Series-Wide Pick

A village mystery, an old church, suspicious locals, and just enough darkness around the edges. Kembleford may have an alarming murder rate, but there is usually still time for tea.

BritBox 🍂🍂 Cosy with a little chill
13
Grantchester
Series-Wide Pick

Picturesque villages, old churches, and country lanes with murders hiding beneath an otherwise peaceful surface make Grantchester an easy autumn watch.

PBS Masterpiece 🍂🍂 Quiet village, troubling secrets
12
Midsomer Murders — "Dark Autumn"
Series 4

Even the title belongs on an October watchlist. Country lanes, isolated villages, and that peculiar feeling that something unpleasant is hiding behind the hedgerow make this a particularly good Midsomer choice for the season.

Acorn TV 🍂🍂🍂 Peak Midsomer autumn
11
Agatha Christie's Poirot — "Hallowe'en Party"
Series 12, 2010

If you watch only one Poirot episode in October, this is the obvious choice. A Halloween gathering turns sinister, and Poirot finds himself investigating secrets, old crimes, and a particularly unsettling celebration.

BritBox 🎃🎃🎃 A proper Christie Halloween

Getting Atmospheric

Folklore, isolated houses, and village traditions that have clearly gone wrong.

10
Midsomer Murders — "The Straw Woman"
Series 7

Few things say "everything is about to go terribly wrong in a picturesque village" quite like an old local ritual. Folklore and murder come together in one of Midsomer's most atmospheric stories.

Acorn TV 🔥🎃🎃 Village tradition gone very wrong
9
Midsomer Murders — "The House in the Woods"
Series 9

An isolated house. A dark wood. Midsomer. Do we really need to explain why this belongs here?

Acorn TV 🌲🌲🌲 Do not go into the woods
8
Jonathan Creek — "Ghost's Forge"
Series 3, 1999

Jonathan Creek is practically designed for October viewing: impossible crimes, strange houses, and mysteries that initially appear supernatural.

BritBox 🕯️🕯️🕯️ Impossible mystery territory
7
Magpie Murders
Series-Wide Pick

A dead mystery writer, a missing chapter, and a fictional English village mystery that may hold clues to a very real murder. It has all the ingredients for a chilly October binge.

PBS Masterpiece 📚🕯️🕯️ A mystery inside a mystery

Turning Eerie

Vintage horror homage, past crimes resurfacing, and country houses with a past.

6
Midsomer Murders — "Death and the Divas"
Series 15

Classic horror films meet Midsomer. The episode plays with vintage horror imagery and theatrical scares while delivering exactly the eccentric village mystery Midsomer fans expect.

Acorn TV 🎬👻👻 Old-school spooky fun
5
Jonathan Creek — "The Grinning Man"
Special, 2009

A mysterious old house and a room with a particularly disturbing history give this feature-length mystery everything you want from an October night.

BritBox 🕯️👻👻👻 The house is definitely not fine
4
Moonflower Murders
Series-Wide Pick

A disappearance leads Susan Ryeland back into the world of murder mysteries, old crimes, and secrets hidden inside a book. With an English country hotel, a past murder, and two timelines to unravel, it has terrific dark-evening atmosphere.

PBS Masterpiece 🌙🕯️🕯️🕯️ Secrets hiding in plain sight

Lights On

The three most unabashedly Halloween-coded picks on the list.

3
Jonathan Creek — "Danse Macabre"
Series 2, 1998

This one actually takes place at Halloween. A bestselling writer is murdered, a skeleton-costumed killer appears to vanish, and Maddy faces one of the show's wonderfully bizarre impossible crimes.

BritBox 🎃🎃🎃🎃🎃 Halloween mystery perfection
2
Midsomer Murders — "Things That Go Bump in the Night"
Series 8, 2004

A séance, a supposedly haunted house, and a village filled with people who may — or may not — believe in things that go bump in the night. This Midsomer mystery leans beautifully into supernatural atmosphere while still delivering the eccentric characters and village secrets fans expect.

Acorn TV 👻🕯️🕯️ A haunted house, or so they say
1
Jonathan Creek — "Satan's Chimney"
Special, 2001

Even by Jonathan Creek standards, the title sounds ominous. An elaborate impossible mystery and gothic surroundings make this exactly the sort of story to save for a dark October night.

BritBox 🌑🌑🌑🌑🌑 Lights staying on
