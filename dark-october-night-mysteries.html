13 British Mysteries for a Dark October Night

The nights are getting longer, the kettle's on, and somewhere in a quiet English village, something is definitely not quite right. A countdown from cosy cases with a hint of autumn unease to proper lights-on-territory viewing.

Start the Countdown →
Jump to Start Cosy (13–10) Getting Atmospheric (9–6) Turning Eerie (5–3) Lights On (2–1) Where to Watch

Curated and verified by British TV Hub · Streaming availability checked September 2026

For October, we've gathered 13 British mysteries made for dark evenings — a mix of standout episodes, specials, and series perfect for seasonal viewing. Start at No. 13 if you want cosy. Keep going if you dare.

Start Cosy

Comfort viewing with just enough autumn chill to feel seasonal.

13
Grantchester
Series 1, Episode 3

An elderly parishioner tells Sidney that someone is trying to kill her — and dies not long after. What looks like a quiet natural death unravels into a case built on old grudges, inheritance, and secrets the family would rather keep buried.

PBS Masterpiece 🍂🍂 Quiet village, unsettling confession
12
Midsomer Murders — "Ghosts of Christmas Past"
Series 7, Episode 7

Technically a Christmas episode, but the title and haunted-family atmosphere earn it a spot here anyway. A family reunion turns into a reckoning with old secrets and a death nine years in the making — no literal ghosts required.

Acorn TV 🍂👻 A family haunted by its own past
11
Midsomer Murders — "Death and the Divas"
Series 15, Episode 4

Classic horror films meet Midsomer. The episode plays with vintage horror imagery and theatrical scares while delivering exactly the eccentric village mystery Midsomer fans expect.

Acorn TV 🎬👻👻 Old-school spooky fun
10
Dalgliesh — "Death in Holy Orders, Part 1"
Series 3, Episode 1

A remote Anglo-Catholic theological college, an unresolved scandal, and an archdeacon found dead in the chapel — one of Dalgliesh's most atmospheric, cloistered mysteries.

Acorn TV 🕯️🕯️ Sombre and slow-burning

Getting Atmospheric

Folklore, isolated houses, and village traditions that have clearly gone wrong.

9
Midsomer Murders — "Death and Dreams"
Series 6, Episode 2

A darker-toned Midsomer entry that earns its place on title and mood alone. What looks like a suicide unravels into a disturbing family history — nothing supernatural, but the late-night tone is exactly right.

Acorn TV 🌒🌒 Dark tone, late-night viewing
8
Miss Scarlet & The Duke — "The Vanishing"
Series 3, Episode 1

A magician vanishes mid-trick during his farewell performance at London's Flanders Theatre, and the venue's owner insists it's the work of the "Flanders Phantom." Eliza doesn't believe in ghosts — but she does believe someone's hiding something.

PBS Masterpiece 🎩👻 A theatre curse, or so they claim
7
Midsomer Murders — "Dark Autumn"
Series 4, Episode 5

Even the title belongs on an October watchlist. Country lanes, isolated villages, and that peculiar feeling that something unpleasant is hiding behind the hedgerow make this a particularly good Midsomer choice for the season.

Acorn TV 🍂🍂🍂 Peak Midsomer autumn
6
Dalgliesh — "The Black Tower, Part 1"
Series 1, 2021

Dalgliesh travels to a clifftop home for the disabled in Dorset after an old friend dies suddenly — and finds a second, more suspicious death waiting for him. One of the moodier, more gothic Dalgliesh mysteries, with isolation and secrets that don't stay buried.

Acorn TV 🌊🏚️ Isolated and quietly gothic

Turning Eerie

Vintage horror homage, past crimes resurfacing, and manipulated messages from beyond the grave.

5
Midsomer Murders — "The House in the Woods"
Series 9, Episode 1

An isolated house. A dark wood. Midsomer. Do we really need to explain why this belongs here?

Acorn TV 🌲🌲🌲 Do not go into the woods
4
Miss Scarlet & The Duke — "Memento Mori"
Series 1, Episode 4

A death photographer starts receiving manipulated photographs that appear to carry messages from beyond the grave. It looks like a haunting — until Eliza traces it back to a very human, very calculated act of revenge.

PBS Masterpiece 📷👻 A ghost story with a human culprit
3
Midsomer Murders — "The Straw Woman"
Series 7, Episode 6

Few things say "everything is about to go terribly wrong in a picturesque village" quite like an old local ritual. Folklore and murder come together in one of Midsomer's most atmospheric stories.

Acorn TV 🔥🎃🎃 Village tradition gone very wrong

Lights On

The two most unabashedly Halloween-coded picks on the list.

2
Midsomer Murders — "Things That Go Bump in the Night"
Series 8, Episode 1

A séance, a supposedly haunted house, and a village filled with people who may — or may not — believe in things that go bump in the night. This Midsomer mystery leans beautifully into supernatural atmosphere while still delivering the eccentric characters and village secrets fans expect.

Acorn TV 👻🕯️🕯️ A haunted house, or so they say
1
Agatha Christie's Poirot — "Hallowe'en Party"
Series 12, 2010

The obvious finale: if you watch only one Poirot episode in October, this is the one. An actual Halloween party turns sinister, and Poirot finds himself investigating secrets, old crimes, and a particularly unsettling celebration.

BritBox 🎃🎃🎃🎃🎃 A proper Christie Halloween
