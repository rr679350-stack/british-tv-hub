The List

8 British Cozy Mysteries Worth Your Time

Cozy mystery means minimal violence, an amateur or unconventional detective, and a setting you'd want to visit. Here's where to start.

01
The Modern Classic

The Thursday Murder Club — Richard Osman

Four retirees investigate cold cases from their retirement village. The book that relaunched the modern cozy mystery boom, now also a Netflix film.

Find it on Amazon →
02
The Riverside Pick

The Marlow Murder Club — Robert Thorogood

A crossword setter, a dog-walker, and a vicar's wife solve a murder on the Thames. From the creator of Death in Paradise.

Find it on Amazon →
03
The Original Village Whodunit

The Killings at Badger's Drift — Caroline Graham

The novel behind Midsomer Murders. Named one of the CWA's Top 100 Crime Novels of All Time.

Find it on Amazon →
04
The Clergyman Detective

Sidney Chambers and the Shadow of Death — James Runcie

The book behind Grantchester. A 1950s vicar with a knack for solving crimes the police can't.

Set in Quebec rather than Britain, but beloved by the same cozy-mystery readers for its warmth and village feel.
