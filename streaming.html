How to Watch British TV in the United States

Navigating British streaming can feel overwhelming. Here is exactly where to find the best shows, without the guesswork — from free options to essential paid subscriptions.

All prices verified May 2026  ·  Free and paid options  ·  No affiliate links
Paid Subscriptions

The Essential British TV Services

These are the services that serious British TV fans actually use. Each has a distinct library — knowing which one has your show saves a lot of time.

01
The Essential Subscription

BritBox

The largest library of classic and contemporary British TV available in the US. Inspector Morse, Vera, Doc Martin, Death in Paradise and thousands more. The first subscription any serious British TV fan should get.

From $8.99/month  ·  Free with some cable packages
Inspector Morse, Vera, Miss Marple, Doc Martin, Death in Paradise, Heartbeat, The Bill and thousands more
02
The Mystery Lover's Choice

Acorn TV

Specialises in British and international mysteries. Home to Hinterland, Agatha Raisin, Murdoch Mysteries, and many exclusive US premieres. An excellent complement to BritBox, especially for cosy mystery fans.

From $6.99/month  ·  Free trial available
Hinterland, The Detectorists, Hope Street, Agatha Raisin, Murdoch Mysteries, Jack Irish
03
Public Broadcasting

PBS Passport

PBS Passport gives you extended access to PBS content including Masterpiece, where many BBC dramas air first in the US. If you watch Downton Abbey, Victoria, or Sherlock, this is worth having.

From $5/month (membership donation)  ·  Supports public broadcasting
Masterpiece Theatre, Downton Abbey, Victoria, Sherlock, Call the Midwife, Wolf Hall
04
Worth Checking

Netflix & Amazon Prime

Both carry British content, though libraries change frequently. Netflix has Broadchurch, The Crown, and selected BBC titles. Amazon has some ITV content and originals like Good Omens. Check before subscribing specifically for British TV.

Netflix from $6.99/month  ·  Prime from $8.99/month
Netflix: Broadchurch, The Crown, Black Mirror  ·  Prime: Good Omens, Downton Abbey (older series)
Free Options

Watch British TV for Free in the US

You don't need a paid subscription to start. These free services carry a surprising amount of quality British content.

Free with Ads

Tubi

Tubi offers a surprisingly large free library supported by ads, including older series of Inspector Morse, Midsomer Murders, and various classic British dramas. A great place to start.

Free with Ads

Pluto TV

Has dedicated British TV channels running classic shows around the clock. Good for casual viewing and discovery — you can't choose specific episodes but it's always on.

Free with Library Card

Kanopy

Many public libraries offer Kanopy for free, which carries British films and some series. Check if your library card gives you access — it costs you nothing.
