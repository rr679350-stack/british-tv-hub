Free Options

Watch British TV for Free in the US

You don't need a paid subscription to start. These free services carry a surprising amount of quality British content.

Free with Ads

Tubi

Tubi offers a surprisingly large free library supported by ads, including older series of Inspector Morse, Midsomer Murders, and various classic British dramas. A great place to start.

Free with Ads

Pluto TV

Has dedicated British TV channels running classic shows around the clock. Good for casual viewing and discovery — you can't choose specific episodes but it's always on.

Free with Library Card

Kanopy

Many public libraries offer Kanopy for free, which carries British films and some series. Check if your library card gives you access — it costs you nothing.
