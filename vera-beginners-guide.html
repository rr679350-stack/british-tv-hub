New to Vera?

Fourteen series, one unforgettable detective, and a complete story now that Brenda Blethyn has hung up the coat. Here's everything you need to know before you press play.

What Is Vera About?

Vera follows DCI Vera Stanhope, a brilliant, rumpled detective investigating murders across the wild coastline and villages of Northumberland. Each episode is a feature-length, self-contained mystery, driven as much by Vera's own character as by the case itself.

SettingNorthumberland, in the north east of England
FormatFeature-length, self-contained mysteries
First aired2011
Based onAnn Cleeves' Vera Stanhope novels
StatusComplete — ended with Series 14 in January 2025

Where Should a Beginner Start?

Start at the Beginning

Series 1, Episode 1 · “Hidden Depths”

The pilot, introducing DCI Vera Stanhope and DS Joe Ashworth. Since the show is now complete, watching in broadcast order from here is the simplest way through all 14 series.

One quirk worth knowing: the pilot doesn't adapt Ann Cleeves' first Vera Stanhope novel. It adapts her third, also called Hidden Depths. If you plan to read the books too, note that the TV and book series don't run in the same order.

Vera's Sergeants

DS Joe Ashworth (David Leon)

Series 1–4, 13–14

Vera's original sergeant, and her closest thing to a surrogate son. Left the show after Series 4, then returned for the final two series.

DS Aiden Healy (Kenny Doughty)

Series 5–12

Steps in as Vera's sergeant for the show's middle stretch, becoming just as trusted a partner as Ashworth before him.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the first episode of Vera?

“Hidden Depths,” which aired in 2011. It introduces DCI Vera Stanhope and DS Joe Ashworth and is the best starting point for most new viewers.

Does Vera need to be watched in book order or TV order?

They're not the same order. The TV pilot adapts Ann Cleeves' third Vera Stanhope novel, “Hidden Depths,” rather than her first, “The Crow Trap.” New viewers can simply follow TV broadcast order without worrying about the books.

Who are Vera's sergeants?

DS Joe Ashworth (David Leon) appears in Series 1–4, then returns for the final two series, 13 and 14. DS Aiden Healy (Kenny Doughty) is Vera's sergeant in between, across Series 5–12.

Do I need to watch Vera in order?

No. Each episode is a self-contained mystery. Watching in order adds context, mainly around the sergeant handovers, but isn't essential.

Is Vera still being made?

No. Vera concluded with Series 14 in January 2025, after Brenda Blethyn chose to leave the role. The finale, “The Dark Wives,” shares its title with Ann Cleeves' most recent Vera Stanhope novel.

How many series of Vera are there?

14 series in total, all available to stream on BritBox in the US.

Where can I watch Vera in the US?

BritBox is Vera's main US streaming home, with all 14 series available.

Is Vera based on books?

Yes, on Ann Cleeves' Vera Stanhope novels, 11 books in total starting with “The Crow Trap.” The show has outpaced the books, adapting original stories once it caught up to the source material.
