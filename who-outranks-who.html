Who Outranks Who?

DC, DS, DI, DCI — who actually outranks who? A quick, friendly key to the initials every British mystery assumes you already know.

Even Sherlock plays fast and loose with rank — useful to know before you start shouting "Detective" at the screen.

Curated and verified by British TV Hub

The Bit Everyone Misses

"Detective" isn't a higher rank — it's a specialism. A DC is the same rank as a PC. A DS is the same rank as a Sergeant. It just means plainclothes investigation instead of uniformed patrol.

Did You Notice?

Characters almost never get their full title said out loud. You won't hear "Detective Chief Inspector Stanhope" — you'll hear a surname on a case board, then someone shouting "Guv" down a corridor.

Spotted On Screen

A few familiar faces, now that the initials make sense.

DCI

Vera Stanhope

Runs every case in Vera from the top, with a rotating cast of sergeants doing the legwork beside her.

DCI

John Luther

Idris Elba's Serious Crime Unit detective is one of the rank's most recognisable modern faces.

DCI → Det. Superintendent

Jane Tennison

Fights for a DCI's respect early on, then climbs to Detective Superintendent as Prime Suspect goes on.

DI

Jimmy Pérez

Heads up investigations across Shetland, later sharing the role with DI Ruth Calder.

DI

Alec Hardy

Arrives in Broadchurch as an outsider DI, paired with the town's own DS Ellie Miller.

DS → DI

Robbie Lewis

Spends Inspector Morse as a sergeant, then steps up to DI by the time he's leading his own spin-off.

DS → DI

James Hathaway

Lewis's sergeant for years, promoted to DI in the show's closing stretch.

DC

Endeavour Morse

Just starting out as a DC in Endeavour, decades before he becomes the DCI everyone knows from the original series.

DCI & DI

Cassie Stuart & Sunny Khan

Led Unforgotten's cold case unit as DCI and DI, without the usual senior-junior friction — just two equals working well together.

DI

Humphrey Goodman

Arrives on Saint Marie as a DI in Death in Paradise, working alongside his DS.

DS

Camille Bordey

The local DS who keeps every visiting British inspector grounded through Death in Paradise's early series.

DCI

Tom & John Barnaby

Both cousins hold the same rank across Midsomer Murders' entire run — a DCI is always the one touring the village fetes.
