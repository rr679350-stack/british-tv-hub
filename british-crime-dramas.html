Best British Crime Dramas

Not a case-of-the-week and not a cozy village whodunit — these are the flagship serialized crime dramas that built their reputation one long, tense story at a time.

Curated and verified by British TV Hub · Streaming availability checked September 2026

What Sets These Apart?

These shows build one long, serialized story rather than solving a fresh case every episode, and they lean into psychological weight and moral complexity rather than the tidy comfort of a cozy mystery.

Line of Duty

AC-12, a fictional anti-corruption unit, investigates police officers suspected of corruption within their own force. Six series build to one of British TV's most talked-about mysteries, with a seventh coming in 2027.

Full Guide →
Happy Valley

Sarah Lancashire won two BAFTAs as Sergeant Catherine Cawood in Sally Wainwright's West Yorkshire drama, built around a cat-and-mouse arc with her nemesis across three series and nine years.

Full Guide →
Unforgotten

Nicola Walker and Sanjeev Bhaskar led the first four series investigating decades-old cold cases; Sinéad Keenan has since stepped in opposite Bhaskar, with a seventh series already confirmed.

Full Guide →
Shetland

Douglas Henshall's DI Jimmy Perez carried the first seven series before handing over to Ashley Jensen, investigating murders within the tight-knit island community of Scotland's Shetland Isles.

Full Guide →
Vera

Brenda Blethyn played DCI Vera Stanhope in every one of the show's 14 series, investigating feature-length cases across the wild Northumberland coast until the show's complete run ended in 2025.

Full Guide →
Blue Lights

Siân Brooke, Martin McCann, Katherine Devlin, and Nathan Braniff play newly qualified PSNI officers navigating frontline policing in post-Troubles Belfast, in a series that won Best Drama at the 2025 BAFTAs.

Full Guide →
Trigger Point

Vicky McClure stars as a Metropolitan Police bomb disposal officer defusing a fresh explosives threat in each series, produced by Jed Mercurio's team, the creative force behind Line of Duty.

What Readers Ask Most

What makes these different from British police procedurals or cozy mysteries?

These shows are built around long-form, serialized storytelling rather than a self-contained case each episode, and they lean into psychological weight and moral complexity rather than puzzle-solving comfort.

Which of these shows are streaming on BritBox?

Line of Duty, Happy Valley, Shetland, Vera, Blue Lights, and Trigger Point are all available on BritBox in the US. Unforgotten streams on PBS Masterpiece.

Do I need to watch these shows in order?

Line of Duty absolutely must be watched in order, since it builds one continuous story. Unforgotten, Happy Valley, Shetland, and Vera can mostly be watched out of order, since each series or case is largely self-contained, though watching in order adds context.

What should I watch after Line of Duty?

Trigger Point is a natural next stop, since it comes from the same production company and stars Line of Duty's Vicky McClure. Happy Valley offers a similarly intense, character-driven kind of crime drama.

Is Shetland based on books?

Yes, on Ann Cleeves' Shetland novels, though the show has diverged from them substantially over its run. See the Shetland reading guide for the full list.
