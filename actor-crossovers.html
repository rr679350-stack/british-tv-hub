British TV Actors You Recognize From Other Mystery Shows

Seen a familiar face while watching a British mystery? The same actors frequently appear across Britain's most popular crime dramas, cozy mysteries and police procedurals. Here are five familiar British TV actors and the roles you may recognize them from.

Jump to James Norton Vicky McClure Ben Miller Kevin Whately Neil Dudgeon

Last fact-checked: September 2026.

James Norton: Happy Valley and Grantchester

Happy Valley (Tommy Lee Royce)→Grantchester (Sidney Chambers)

Remarkably, both shows premiered in 2014, allowing viewers to see Norton as the terrifying Tommy Lee Royce and the warm, doubt-filled Sidney Chambers in the same year.

Happy Valley Guide → Grantchester Guide →

Vicky McClure: Line of Duty and Trigger Point

Line of Duty (DS Kate Fleming)→Trigger Point (Lana Washington)

The two dramas share a creative connection through Jed Mercurio, who created Line of Duty and executive-produced Trigger Point. Trigger Point was created and written by Daniel Brierley.

Line of Duty Guide → Trigger Point Guide →

Ben Miller: Death in Paradise and Professor T

Death in Paradise (DI Richard Poole)→Professor T (Jasper Tempest)

Ben Miller starred as the wonderfully particular DI Richard Poole in the first two series of Death in Paradise before exchanging the Caribbean sunshine for the Cambridge classrooms of Professor T.

Death in Paradise Guide → Professor T Guide →

Kevin Whately: Miss Marple, Inspector Morse and Lewis

Miss Marple (DS Fletcher)→Inspector Morse (Sergeant Lewis)→Lewis (DI Robbie Lewis)

He played Detective Sergeant Fletcher in the 1985 Miss Marple adaptation A Murder Is Announced. Two years later, he debuted as Sergeant Lewis in Inspector Morse, appearing in 32 episodes over 13 years before returning as DI Robbie Lewis in the spin-off Lewis.

Miss Marple Guide → Inspector Morse Guide →

Neil Dudgeon: Inspector Morse and Midsomer Murders

Inspector Morse (Michaels in The Way Through the Woods)→Midsomer Murders (Daniel Bolt in Garden of Death)→Midsomer Murders (DCI John Barnaby)

Before becoming DCI John Barnaby in 2011, Dudgeon appeared in Inspector Morse as Michaels and played landscape gardener Daniel Bolt in the Series 4 Midsomer Murders episode Garden of Death.

Inspector Morse Guide → Midsomer Murders Guide →
