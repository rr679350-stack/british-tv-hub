Meet Tilly, Your British TV Guide

What Is Tilly?

Loving British TV shouldn't mean spending hours hunting across BritBox, Acorn TV, ITVX, and PBS to find where your show is streaming — especially when what's available changes by region, month, and season.

Tilly is here to solve that. She's your personal British TV streaming guide, and she knows her stuff. Ask her anything about finding, watching, or discovering British mysteries, dramas, and crime shows.

Ask Tilly Anything Like:

"Where can I watch Vera?" — She'll tell you which service has it in your region, right now.

"What's on BritBox this month?" — Get the latest arrivals and what's currently streaming.

"I love Happy Valley. What should I watch next?" — Get personalised recommendations based on your taste.

"Can I stream this in the US/UK/Australia?" — Instant answers about regional availability.

"What's a good starter cozy mystery?" — Discover hidden gems you might have missed.

No ads. No dead links. No scrolling through search results. Just ask, and get an answer.
