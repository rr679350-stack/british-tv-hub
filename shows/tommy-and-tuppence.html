Agatha Christie's Tommy & Tuppence

1 Season 6 Episodes Agatha Christie Contemporary Setting BritBox

Agatha Christie's Tommy & Tuppence is the first contemporary, English-language adaptation of Christie's playful detective duo. Antonia Thomas plays Tuppence, an actress, and Josh Dylan plays Tommy Beresford, a crime writer who's lost his spark — a couple who fall in and out of love as readily as they tumble into mysteries, now set in present-day Hampstead. Imelda Staunton plays Tommy's Aunt Ada, a retired private detective, with writing by Phoebe Eclair-Powell throughout the six-episode season. The first two episodes premiered on BritBox in the US and Canada on September 15, 2026 — timed to Agatha Christie's own birthday — with the rest of the season following weekly.

Part of British TV Hub's British TV autumn 2026 preview.

About the show

Tommy and Tuppence Beresford first appeared in Agatha Christie's 1922 novel The Secret Adversary, and went on to feature in four more books and stories through Postern of Fate (1973). This BritBox series is the first time any Christie property has been reimagined as a contemporary, English-language series — a 1983 London Weekend Television series and a 2015 BBC series, Partners in Crime, both kept the story in its original period setting. The series is written by Phoebe Eclair-Powell (her first TV series commission) and produced by Lookout Point, part of BBC Studios, in association with Agatha Christie Limited. Fergus O'Brien directed the first four episodes and Ellie Heydon the final two, with Stella Merz producing.

What Makes the Series Special

A First for Christie

The First Contemporary Christie Adaptation

Earlier screen versions of Tommy and Tuppence — a 1983 London Weekend Television series and the BBC's 2015 Partners in Crime — kept the stories in their original period setting. This is the first time any Agatha Christie property has been reimagined as a present-day, English-language series.

Source Material

Drawn From Five Christie Works

The series draws on Agatha Christie's Tommy and Tuppence novels and short stories: The Secret Adversary (1922), Partners in Crime (1929), N or M? (1941), By the Pricking of My Thumbs (1968), and Postern of Fate (1973).
