Agatha Christie's Tommy & Tuppence

6 Episodes Stars Antonia Thomas Agatha Christie Mystery Drama Contemporary

The first-ever contemporary English-language Agatha Christie adaptation reimagines her playful detective duo for the present day. Antonia Thomas plays the sharp, impulsive Tuppence Cowley and Josh Dylan the loveable try-hard Tommy Beresford — a couple who fall in and out of love as readily as they tumble into mysteries. With Imelda Staunton among the cast and writing by Phoebe Eclair-Powell, it's a fresh, witty spin on two of Christie's most spirited sleuths.
