Sherwood

2 Series Ensemble Cast Nottinghamshire Series 3 Filming

Created by playwright James Graham and loosely inspired by real events in his hometown, Sherwood follows a present-day murder in a former Nottinghamshire mining village that reopens decades-old wounds from the 1984-85 miners' strike. Each series is a single, six-episode ensemble story rather than a case-of-the-week procedural, with a different central mystery per series. Sherwood streams on BritBox in the US — see British TV Hub's guide to where to watch British TV in the US.

Grounded, Politically Charged

Sherwood deals with real historical trauma, undercover policing, and community division alongside its murder plots, with a tone closer to prestige drama than genre mystery. Frequently Asked Questions

Source Material

Is Sherwood based on a true story?

Loosely. Creator James Graham drew on real murders that took place in his hometown of Annesley Woodhouse, Nottinghamshire, using them as inspiration for an otherwise fictional story.
