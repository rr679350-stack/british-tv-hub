Doc Martin

10 Series Stars Martin Clunes Cornwall Comedy Drama Cozy

A brilliant London surgeon develops a sudden phobia of blood and retreats to a sleepy Cornish village as the local GP — where his prickly, tactless bedside manner collides with a cast of eccentric locals. Gorgeous Port Isaac scenery, gentle humour, and a slow-building romance make this one of the most comforting watches in British television. Ten series of pure escapism.
