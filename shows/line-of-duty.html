British Crime Thriller · BBC · 2012–2021

Line of Duty

6 Series Police Corruption Thriller RTS Award-winning Edge-of-seat

AC-12, an anti-corruption unit, hunts bent coppers inside the force — and the result is some of the most nerve-shredding television British TV Hub has come across. Famous for its feature-length interrogation scenes, its acronyms (OCG, anyone?), and cliffhangers that had the whole country theorising about 'H', this is appointment viewing at its most addictive. Jed Mercurio's series rewards bingeing.
