A Missing Roman Bronze

A wealthy antiquities collector is murdered, and Mick and Shazia quickly realize it's far more than a burglary gone wrong. The case takes them from a Mayfair gallery to a forgery workshop and even the House of Lords, as they hunt down a missing Roman bronze. Meanwhile, Mick weighs Rosa's possible move to London while trying to make time to visit his imprisoned, art-forging father, Ron — not easy, given Mick is the one who put him there.
