Father Brown

13 Series 140 Episodes Stars Mark Williams 1950s Cotswolds Priest Sleuth

Mark Williams stars as the mild-mannered Roman Catholic priest of the fictional Cotswold village of Kembleford, whose gentle exterior hides a razor-sharp understanding of human nature. Loosely adapted from G.K. Chesterton's short stories and moved to a fixed 1950s setting, Father Brown has aired 13 series and 140 episodes since 2013, with Series 14 in production and Series 15 already commissioned to follow — one of the BBC's most enduring daytime dramas. Father Brown streams on BritBox in the US — see how it stacks up in British TV Hub's guide to where to watch British TV in the US.

Setting and Filming Locations

Setting & Filming

Blockley, Gloucestershire

The fictional village of Kembleford is filmed in Blockley, a real Cotswold village, with the local Church of St Peter and St Paul standing in as Father Brown's St Mary's, and the former Moreton-in-Marsh hospital used for the police station and Father Brown's kitchen.

Cotswolds, England

Books Behind the Series

Original Stories

G.K. Chesterton's Father Brown Stories

The TV series loosely adapts Chesterton's short stories, which originally ran from 1911 to 1936 with Father Brown travelling the world. The show fixes the setting to 1950s Kembleford instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Origins

Is Father Brown based on a true story or real books?

The character comes from G.K. Chesterton's short stories, first published between 1911 and 1936. The TV series loosely adapts them, moving the setting to a fixed 1950s timeframe in the fictional village of Kembleford.

Quick Answer
SourceG.K. Chesterton stories
Status

Is Father Brown still being made?

Yes. Father Brown has aired 13 series and 140 episodes. Series 14 is in production, and the BBC has also commissioned Series 15.

Quick Answer
StatusOngoing
NextSeries 14 & 15 confirmed
Episode Count

How many episodes of Father Brown are there?

There are exactly 140 episodes across 13 series, with most series running 10 episodes.

Viewing Order

Do I need to watch Father Brown in order?

No. Nearly every episode is a self-contained mystery, so you can start with any series. Character relationships evolve gradually, but the cases themselves don't require prior episodes.
