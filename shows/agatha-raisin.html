Agatha Raisin

4 Series + Pilot 20 Mysteries Stars Ashley Jensen The Cotswolds Based on M.C. Beaton's Novels

Ashley Jensen stars as Agatha Raisin, a brash, glamorous London PR executive who leaves the city behind for the fictional Cotswolds village of Carsely, only to find that murder follows her there. Adapted from M.C. Beaton's long-running novels, the series began with a 2014 Sky One pilot and an eight-episode first series in 2016 before continuing as an Acorn TV production. Four series have aired so far, and a fifth season is in pre-production. Agatha Raisin streams on Acorn TV in the US. Main Characters and Cast

Lead

Ashley Jensen as Agatha Raisin

A sharp-tongued former PR high-flyer who brings her big-city confidence to village life. Vain, impulsive and funny, Agatha is also fiercely loyal, and her refusal to leave a mystery alone drives every story.

The Men in Her Life

James Lacey, Sir Charles Fraith and Roy Silver

Jamie Glover plays James Lacey, Agatha's on-and-off love interest, while Jason Merrells plays the aristocratic Sir Charles Fraith, whose friendship with Agatha carries its own romantic tension. Mathew Horne plays Roy Silver, her loyal former PR colleague.

What Makes the Series Special

1 · The Lead

Ashley Jensen's Agatha

Jensen plays Agatha as gloriously imperfect, mixing comic bluster with real warmth. A heroine who is allowed to be vain, pushy and wrong makes the show stand apart from more polished detectives.

Performance
At a Glance
ToneComic, warm
2 · The Setting

A Picture-Postcard Village

Honey-stone cottages, village fetes and country pubs give every episode a classic Cotswolds look. The setting is half the comfort, and the contrast with Agatha's city glamour fuels much of the humour.

Cotswolds
At a Glance
SettingCarsely (fictional)
3 · The Tone

Lighter Than Most Mysteries

Agatha Raisin leans into comedy and romance, with murders that stay largely off-screen and rarely turn graphic. It suits viewers who want a puzzle without the darkness of a modern police drama.

Light Content

Setting and Filming Locations

Setting & Filming

Carsely, Played by Biddestone

Agatha's home village of Carsely is fictional, but the series filmed its village scenes mainly in Biddestone, a picturesque village in Wiltshire. The books place Carsely in the Cotswolds, with Agatha regularly visiting real towns such as Evesham and Moreton-in-Marsh.

Wiltshire, England
At a Glance
Fictional VillageCarsely
Filmed InBiddestone

Books Behind the Series

Source Novels

M.C. Beaton's Agatha Raisin Mysteries

The series adapts the novels of M.C. Beaton, a pen name of Marion Chesney, beginning with Agatha Raisin and the Quiche of Death in 1992. The series now runs to 36 novels, most recently Sugar and Spite in 2025, with co-author R.W. Green continuing Agatha's adventures.

Episode Count

How many episodes of Agatha Raisin are there?

There are 20 original mysteries, counting the 2014 pilot: eight shorter episodes in 2016, then three later series of three, four and four feature-length stories. Some Acorn TV listings count the pilot as Series 1, Episode 1 and split later stories into two parts, so episode numbers can look different there.
