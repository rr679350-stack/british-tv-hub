Sanditon

3 Series Jane Austen Seaside Resort Period Drama Romance

Based on Jane Austen's final, unfinished novel, Sanditon transports you to a fashionable seaside resort where romance, intrigue, and fortune-hunting abound from the sandy shore to the candlelit assembly rooms. Rose Williams stars as bold, modern heroine Charlotte Heywood alongside Crystal Clarke's heiress Georgiana Lambe. Gorgeous costumes, sparkling wit, love triangles, and addictive drama penned by renowned Austen adapter Andrew Davies — your carriage awaits.

About the show

Jane Austen was working on Sanditon, an eleven-chapter fragment set in a seaside resort town, when she died in 1817 — and Andrew Davies, the screenwriter behind Pride and Prejudice and War & Peace, expanded that fragment into the full series. Rose Williams stars as Charlotte Heywood throughout, opposite Theo James for the first series and, in a supporting role, Kris Marshall (also familiar from Death in Paradise) as Tom Parker. ITV cancelled the show after one series in 2019, but a fan campaign and strong PBS Masterpiece ratings brought it back for two more series, concluding in 2023.
