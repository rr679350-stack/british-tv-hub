The Madame Blanc Mysteries

Season 5 7 Episodes Stars Sally Lindsay Filmed in Malta & Gozo Cozy Mystery

The Madame Blanc Mysteries follows Jean White (Sally Lindsay), a Cheshire antiques dealer who is widowed when her husband Rory dies in what looks like an accident at the couple's cottage in the fictional French village of Sainte Victoire — only for Jean to discover it was murder. Four seasons on, she's turned amateur sleuth alongside local taxi driver Dom Hayes (Steve Edge), solving a new crime roughly every episode. Season 5 premiered on Channel 5 in the UK on October 29, 2026, and is coming to Acorn TV in the US; compare BritBox and Acorn TV to see which fits your watchlist. This season is built around Jean and Dom's wedding plans, with a fresh run of murders determined to get in the way.

About the show

The Madame Blanc Mysteries is an original story created by Sally Lindsay, who stars as Jean White and co-writes the series with Sue Vincent (who also plays local mechanic Gloria Beaushaw). It's a co-production between Clapperboard and Saffron Cherry Productions for Channel 5 in the UK, with AMC Networks' Acorn TV holding the international distribution rights, including in the US. The show is set in the fictional village of Sainte Victoire but is actually filmed on location in Malta and on the island of Gozo. Since its 2021 debut it has built a recurring-guest-star tradition, bringing in well-known British sitcom, soap and panel-show faces for small roles most seasons — Season 5 continues that with its biggest guest list yet.

New Viewer's Guide

Where to Start

Mostly Case-of-the-Week — Start Almost Anywhere

Each episode is largely a self-contained mystery, so The Madame Blanc Mysteries is friendlier to jump into mid-run than most crime dramas. That said, Jean and Dom's relationship and the recurring John Marsden storyline build across seasons, so Season 1 gives the fullest picture of how Jean ended up in Sainte Victoire and why she and Dom work as a pair.

1 · Season 1

The Essential Starting Point

Jean investigates her husband's death and the identity of his mistress, alongside a string of local cases including a stolen painting and a theft at the village church. It's the cleanest introduction to Jean, Dom, and the village of Sainte Victoire, and sets up the emotional hook that keeps a cozy, case-of-the-week show grounded.

What Makes the Series Special

Creator-Led

Created and Co-Written by Its Star

Sally Lindsay created The Madame Blanc Mysteries and co-writes it with castmate Sue Vincent — an unusual level of creative control for a lead actor on a British cozy mystery, and part of why the show's tone feels so consistent season to season.
