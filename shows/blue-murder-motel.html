Blue Murder Motel

Series 1 8 Episodes New Zealand Cozy Mystery Mōwai Bay

Married detectives Vinny and Cole Coleman trade their careers with the Sydney police for early retirement, sinking their savings into a rundown motel in the fictional New Zealand coastal town of Mōwai Bay. The quiet life doesn't last — within a week of opening, a guest on a boys' weekend is found dead in his bathtub, and the couple's old investigative instincts kick back in as one case follows another across Series 1's eight episodes. Blue Murder Motel streams on Acorn TV in the US; compare BritBox and Acorn TV to see which fits your watchlist. Reviewers have likened its light, character-driven tone to The Brokenwood Mysteries and My Life Is Murder, both from the same corner of New Zealand cozy crime.

About the show

Blue Murder Motel was created by Kate McDermott, who also leads the writing team, and Steven Zanoski, who produces; it's made by Great Southern Television for TVNZ, where Series 1 premiered on TVNZ 1 and TVNZ+ on January 18, 2026, running weekly through March 8. Michala Banas and Brett Tucker star as Vanessa "Vinny" and Peter "Cole" Coleman, reuniting on screen after both appearing in the Australian drama McLeod's Daughters years earlier. The supporting cast includes Jayden Daniels as the young local constable Jamie Haira, Jaime McDermott as sharp-tongued motel cleaner Saffron, and Stephanie Tauevihi as long-term motel guest Maxine. Filming took place in Ōrewa, on Auckland's Hibiscus Coast, where the real Edgewater Motel was dressed up as the fictional Blue Motel. The series has already been picked up for a number of international markets, including Acorn TV for the US and Canada and ITV for the UK.

New Viewer's Guide

Where to Start

Start with Episode 1, “Party Boy”

Blue Murder Motel is only one series deep so far, so there's no back catalogue to sort through — start at the beginning. Each episode centers on a new case at or around the motel, but the central relationship between Vinny, Cole, and local constable Jamie Haira builds across the run, so watching in order gets you the most out of it.

What Makes the Series Special

Comfort Viewing

Light Rather Than Grim

Reviewers have described Blue Murder Motel as comfort-food mystery television rather than a dark crime thriller, with humour, coastal scenery, and small-town charm doing a lot of the work alongside the cases themselves.

Setting and Filming Locations

Setting & Filming

Ōrewa, Auckland, New Zealand

Blue Murder Motel is set in the fictional coastal town of Mōwai Bay, but it was filmed in and around Ōrewa, on Auckland's Hibiscus Coast. The real Edgewater Motel, on Ōrewa Beach, was dressed up as the show's fictional Blue Motel for production.
