Cooper and Fry

Season 1 Streaming Season 2 Renewed Stars Robert James-Collier & Mandip Gill Peak District Stephen Booth Novels

Cooper and Fry is a crime drama series adapted from Stephen Booth's bestselling detective novels, starring Robert James-Collier (Downton Abbey) and Mandip Gill (Doctor Who) as DC Ben Cooper and DC Diane Fry, a mismatched pair of detectives working cases around the fictional Peak District town of Edendale. Cooper is a local still finding his footing in the shadow of his late father, a respected officer in the same force; Fry is a guarded newcomer who has arrived from the city under circumstances she isn't eager to explain. The series streams on BritBox in the US — see how it stacks up in British TV Hub's guide to where to watch British TV in the US.

Setting and Filming Locations

Setting & Filming

Peak District on Screen, Ireland on Location

The story is set in the fictional town of Edendale in Derbyshire's Peak District, but production actually filmed in Ireland — mainly around County Wicklow, with additional filming in County Kildare — using the area's mountains and countryside to stand in for Booth's Derbyshire setting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Source Material

Is Cooper and Fry based on real books?

Yes. The series adapts Stephen Booth's Cooper & Fry novels, an 18-book crime series set in Derbyshire's Peak District. Season 1 covers four of the early novels.

Quick Answer
SourceStephen Booth novels
Streaming

Where can I watch Cooper and Fry in the US?

BritBox is the exclusive US and Canada home for Cooper and Fry. Season 1 arrived for BritBox Premier subscribers in August 2026, with the standard BritBox release following in September 2026.

How many episodes are in Season 1?

In the UK, Season 1 aired as four feature-length stories. For its US release, BritBox presents the same season as eight roughly 45-minute episodes.

Has Cooper and Fry been renewed for Season 2?

Yes. Season 2, an eight-episode run adapting four more Stephen Booth novels, has already filmed in the UK and is expected in 2027.

The story is set in the fictional town of Edendale in Derbyshire's Peak District, but filming actually took place in Ireland, mainly around County Wicklow and County Kildare.

Reading Order

Do I need to read the novels before watching?

No. The series stands on its own, though the books offer a deeper look at Ben Cooper and Diane Fry's characters across many more cases than the show has adapted so far.
