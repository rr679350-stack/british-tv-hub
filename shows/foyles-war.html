Foyle's War

28 Feature-Length Episodes 8 UK Series Stars Michael Kitchen Wartime Hastings Created by Anthony Horowitz

Michael Kitchen stars as Detective Chief Superintendent Christopher Foyle, a quietly principled policeman solving murders in Hastings while the Second World War rages across the Channel. Created by Anthony Horowitz, the series follows the home front year by year from May 1940, then moves into the uneasy post-war world of 1946 and 1947, across 28 feature-length episodes broadcast on ITV from 2002 to 2015. The episodes are grouped into eight series in the UK, while some US listings divide them into nine seasons. Foyle's War streams on Acorn TV in the US. Where to Start

The First Episode, The German Woman (2002)

Set in May 1940, the opener finds Foyle frustrated at being kept on home duties while the war effort calls, until the death of an influential man's German-born wife lands on his desk. It introduces his partnership with driver Sam Stewart and the show's thoughtful, morally serious tone.

Good to Know

Foyle's War in Order: From Wartime to Post-War

Series 1–6 follow the home front in Hastings from 1940 to the end of the war in 1945. Series 7 and 8 move to London in 1946 and 1947, with Foyle drawn into the world of MI5 and the early Cold War.

What Makes the Series Special

1 · The Lead

Michael Kitchen's Understated Foyle

Kitchen does more with a pause or a raised eyebrow than many actors do with a speech. His restrained performance gives the series its calm, intelligent centre.

Performance
At a Glance
ToneQuiet, principled
2 · The History

The Home Front Brought to Life

Each mystery is built around a real aspect of wartime Britain, from internment and rationing to evacuees, the Land Army and American GIs. Viewers come away knowing far more about the period than when they started.

Wartime Britain
At a Glance
Era1940–1947
3 · The Writing

Anthony Horowitz's Craft

Horowitz created the series and wrote many of its episodes, layering moral questions into fair-play puzzles. He went on to write the Susan Ryeland novels, the source of PBS Masterpiece's Marble Hall Murders.

Setting and Filming Locations

Setting & Filming

Wartime Hastings, Then Post-War London

The first six series are set in the Sussex seaside town of Hastings, with a house on Croft Road serving as Foyle's home. When the story moves to London for Series 7 and 8, filming took place in London and Ireland.

How many episodes of Foyle's War are there?

There are 28 feature-length episodes, broadcast on ITV from 2002 to 2015. They are grouped into eight series in the UK, while some US listings divide the same episodes into nine seasons.

Status

Is Foyle's War coming back?

No new episodes have been announced. The final episode aired on January 18, 2015. ITV originally cancelled the series after its fifth series, but later revived it, and Foyle returned for additional post-war stories.

Viewing Order

Should I watch Foyle's War in order?

Yes. The series moves forward through the war year by year, from May 1940 to early 1947, and the characters' lives change along the way.

Is Foyle's War based on true events?

The murder cases are fictional, but each one is set against real aspects of life on the home front, such as rationing, internment, the Land Army and the arrival of American troops.
