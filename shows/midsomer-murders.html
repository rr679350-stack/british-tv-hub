Midsomer Murders

25 Series English Villages Cozy Mystery Long-runner DCI Barnaby

The most genteel-looking fictional county in England may also be its most murderous — and DCI Barnaby has spent decades untangling the homicides hidden behind Midsomer's chocolate-box cottages and flower shows. Wildly inventive in its methods of dispatch yet utterly cozy in tone, it's the ultimate comfort mystery: pour a cuppa, settle in, and try to spot the killer before the credits.

Longevity

Nearly 30 Years on Air

Inspired by Caroline Graham's seven-novel Chief Inspector Barnaby series, the television drama quickly moved beyond its original source material, running to 144 episodes across 25 series and export to more than 200 territories worldwide. A twenty-sixth series, comprising four new mysteries, has been commissioned.
