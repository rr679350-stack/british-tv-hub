Dalgliesh

3 Seasons Bertie Carvel P.D. James Adaptation 1970s Setting Season 4 Renewed

Bertie Carvel plays P.D. James's brooding detective-poet Commander Adam Dalgliesh in this atmospheric period adaptation, set in 1970s England and shadowed throughout by Dalgliesh's private grief over the death of his wife. The first three seasons each adapt three of James's novels across six hour-long episodes, unfolding at the same unhurried, literary pace as the books themselves; Season 4 will bring the show's first original, non-novel story. Dalgliesh streams on Acorn TV in the US — see British TV Hub's guide to where to watch British TV in the US.

Setting and Filming Locations

Setting & Filming

1970s England, Filmed Across the UK

Dalgliesh is set across 1970s England, with each novel's story moving to a new location — a nursing school, a Dorset care home, a London church, a Kent nuclear power station. Season 3 filmed in Northern Ireland, doubling for the English settings of its source novels.

Books Behind the Series

Source Novels

P.D. James's Adam Dalgliesh Series

Dalgliesh has adapted three P.D. James novels per season through its first three seasons: Season 1 covers Shroud for a Nightingale, The Black Tower, and A Taste for Death; Season 3 covers Death in Holy Orders, Cover Her Face, and Devices and Desires. Season 4, set in 1982, adapts two more James novels (including A Mind to Murder) and closes with the series' first entirely original story, written with the blessing of the P.D. James Estate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Source Material

Is Dalgliesh based on real novels?

Yes. Dalgliesh adapts P.D. James's acclaimed Adam Dalgliesh detective novels, three per season, each told across two episodes.

Quick Answer

Status

Is there a Dalgliesh Season 4?

Yes. Season 4 is set in 1982 and greenlit for 2027, with six episodes covering three cases. Two are adapted from P.D. James novels and the third is the series' first entirely original story. Dalgliesh is joined by new colleague DI Clive Roscoe (Sam Swainsbury) rather than Kate Miskin.

How many episodes of Dalgliesh are there?

Is this the first TV Dalgliesh?

No. An earlier ITV/Masterpiece adaptation starring Roy Marsden as Dalgliesh aired from 1983 to 1998. This is a fresh, unconnected adaptation.
