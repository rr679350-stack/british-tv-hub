The Chelsea Detective

4 Seasons Adrian Scarborough Houseboat Detective Season 4 Airing Now

Adrian Scarborough stars as DI Max Arnold, a dyslexic, piano-playing detective who investigates murders among London's wealthy Chelsea set while living modestly aboard a battered houseboat on the Thames — a deliberate contrast the show returns to again and again. Each season tells four self-contained, feature-length cases, with Max's own family life, including his separation from wife Astrid, running quietly alongside the weekly mysteries. The Chelsea Detective streams on Acorn TV in the US — see British TV Hub's guide to where to watch British TV in the US.
