All Creatures Great and Small

6 Series 42 Episodes Stars Nicholas Ralph & Samuel West Yorkshire Dales Found Family

A gorgeous, gentle remake of the classic 1978 series, following newly qualified vet James Herriot (Nicholas Ralph) as he joins Siegfried Farnon's veterinary practice in 1930s Yorkshire. The scenery is breathtaking, the animals are adorable, and the found-family warmth between James, the irascible Siegfried, wayward Tristan, and the household at Skeldale House is irresistible. Based on James Herriot's own memoirs of his life as a Yorkshire vet, the series streams on PBS Masterpiece in the US — see British TV Hub's guide to where to watch British TV in the US for everything else worth watching.

Is This Series for You?

Best For

Fans of Gentle, Feel-Good Drama

There's no murder, no mystery — just warm, character-driven storytelling about a country vet, his eccentric employer, and the animals and farmers of the Yorkshire Dales. If you want comfort television with a bit of a lump-in-the-throat moment each episode, this is it.

What Makes the Series Special

Real Roots

Based on a Real Vet's Life

James Herriot was the pen name of Alf Wight, a real Yorkshire vet whose own decades of practice inspired the memoirs behind this adaptation. That authenticity gives the show's warmth a genuine foundation rather than manufactured sentiment.

True Story
At a Glance
Real NameAlf Wight
Practiced1940–approx. 1990
Setting as Character

The Yorkshire Dales

Filmed on location across the real Yorkshire Dales the books are set in — the sweeping countryside is as much a part of the show's appeal as any character, and it's rarely looked more beautiful on screen.

Yorkshire Dales
At a Glance
SettingYorkshire Dales
Tone

Gentle, Hopeful Storytelling

There's not a cynical bone in its body. Even its harder moments — animal illness, wartime separation, financial hardship — are handled with warmth rather than misery, making it a genuine comfort watch.

Setting and Filming Locations

Fictional Village

Grassington as Darrowby

The village of Grassington, in the Yorkshire Dales, stands in for the fictional village of Darrowby, with its cobbled square transformed into period-era Darrowby for filming in nearly every episode.

Grassington
At a Glance
VillageGrassington
PlaysDarrowby
Countryside

Malham, Swaledale & Beyond

The wider Yorkshire Dales National Park supplies the sweeping countryside shots, including Malham and Malham Cove, the Swaledale valley, Bolton Abbey Estate, and Nidderdale. Broughton Hall Estate near Skipton stands in as Mrs. Pumphrey's grand home.

Yorkshire Dales National Park
At a Glance
LandmarksMalham Cove, Swaledale
Mrs. Pumphrey's HomeBroughton Hall Estate
Real Skeldale House

The Real Thirsk Connection

The real Skeldale House — where Alf Wight actually lived and worked as a vet — still stands at 23 Kirkgate in Thirsk, now open to visitors as the World of James Herriot museum. It isn't used for filming, but it's the true origin of the story.

Thirsk
At a Glance

LocationThirsk, North Yorkshire

Books Behind the Series

Original Stories

James Herriot's Own Memoirs

The series is based on the real memoirs of Alf Wight, published under the pen name James Herriot. The books "If Only They Could Talk" (1970) and "It Shouldn't Happen to a Vet" (1972) were combined for US readers into the omnibus edition titled All Creatures Great and Small.

Frequently Asked Questions

True Story

Is All Creatures Great and Small based on a true story?

Yes. It's based on the memoirs of Alf Wight, a real Yorkshire vet who wrote under the pen name James Herriot, practicing in the Yorkshire Dales from 1940 until his retirement from full-time practice around 1990.

Quick Answer
Based OnReal memoirs
Real NameAlf Wight
Naming

Is this the same as the 1978 BBC series?

No — this is a brand-new 2020 adaptation with an entirely different cast, though it's based on the same James Herriot books as the original 1978–1990 BBC series.

Quick Answer
2020 VersionNew cast & production
SharedSame source books
Status

Is the show still going?

Yes. All Creatures Great and Small is an ongoing series — six series have aired since 2020, and it's already been renewed through an eighth series.

Quick Answer

Cast Change

Why does Mrs. Pumphrey look different after Series 1?

Dame Diana Rigg died in September 2020, shortly after the series premiered. Patricia Hodge assumed the role beginning with Series 2.
