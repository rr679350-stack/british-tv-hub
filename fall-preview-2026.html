Agatha Christie's Tommy & Tuppence

A closer look at the couple headlining BritBox's autumn slate — the cast, the century-old source material, and why this adaptation is a first for Christie.

A First for Christie

Tommy & Tuppence is the first time any Agatha Christie property has been reimagined as a contemporary, English-language series. Previous adaptations — including the 1983 Partners in Crime and a 2015 update — kept the story in its original period setting. This version moves Tommy Beresford and Tuppence into present-day London, keeping the playful chemistry and amateur-detective spirit that made the pair so different from Christie's other creations.

It's a six-episode season, with two episodes airing on premiere day and one new episode arriving weekly after that.

From the Page: The Secret Adversary

Tommy and Tuppence first appeared in Christie's 1922 novel The Secret Adversary — her second published book, and the one that introduced two amateur detectives-for-hire with more nerve than experience. Unlike Poirot or Marple, the Beresfords aged in real time across Christie's novels: they're unmarried and just starting out here, newlyweds by the following book, and grandparents by the time of her final Tommy & Tuppence novel. This new series draws its central relationship from that same source, even as it updates the setting entirely.

Tommy & Tuppence's September 15 premiere anchors a month-long Eras of Agatha Christie celebration on BritBox. A run of classic Marple and Poirot films joins the platform that same day, sorted into five themed collections:

Murder with a change of scenery — A Caribbean Mystery (1983), Murder Ahoy (1964)

Her Darker Side

What Readers Ask Most

What new British shows are premiering in autumn 2026?

The headline autumn premiere is Agatha Christie's Tommy & Tuppence on BritBox (September 15). Also arriving: Ludwig Season 2 and Death Valley Season 2 on BritBox, Blue Murder Motel and The Assassin on Acorn TV, and The Marlow Murder Club Season 3, Marble Hall Murders, and Maigret Season 2 on PBS Masterpiece.

When does Tommy & Tuppence premiere?

September 15, 2026 on BritBox, timed to Agatha Christie's own birthday. It's a six-part series, with two episodes airing on premiere day and one new episode weekly after that.

Are Marlow Murder Club and Marble Hall Murders airing at the same time?

Both premiere September 6, 2026 on PBS Masterpiece, back-to-back on the same Sunday night, and run weekly through October 11.
