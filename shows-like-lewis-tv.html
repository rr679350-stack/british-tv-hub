Shows Like Lewis

Nine series of Robbie Lewis and James Hathaway working cases among the colleges of Oxford turned the Inspector Morse sequel into a much-loved series of its own. Here are eight British detective dramas with the same unhurried, feature-length mysteries and a partnership at their heart, plus where to stream each one in the US.

Explore the List →

The Short Answer

  1. Inspector George Gently — an old-school detective and his younger sergeant police a changing North East England in the 1960s. (Acorn TV)
  2. DCI Banks — a dedicated Yorkshire detective and his team take on serious crimes, based on Peter Robinson's novels. (BritBox)
  3. A Touch of Frost — David Jason's scruffy, stubborn detective solves cases in the fictional town of Denton. (BritBox)
  4. The Inspector Lynley Mysteries — an aristocratic inspector and his working-class sergeant clash and cooperate on murder cases. (BritBox)
  5. Dalgliesh — P.D. James's poet-detective investigates murders in 1970s England. (Acorn TV)
  6. Vienna Blood — a young doctor studying under Freud helps a police detective solve murders in 1900s Vienna. (PBS Masterpiece)
  7. Van der Valk — a sharp Amsterdam detective and his team solve feature-length cases around the city. (PBS Masterpiece)
  8. Karen Pirie — a young Scottish detective reopens cold cases, based on Val McDermid's novels. (BritBox)

Inspector George Gently

Inspector George Gently

2007–2017 · BBC · Acorn TV

Martin Shaw plays a principled senior detective working in the North East of England in the 1960s, paired with an ambitious young sergeant played by Lee Ingleby. The relationship between an older, steady detective and a younger partner with different instincts will feel familiar to anyone who loved Lewis and Hathaway.

Acorn TV

DCI Banks

DCI Banks

2010–2016 · ITV · BritBox

Stephen Tompkinson stars as a dedicated Yorkshire detective in this adaptation of Peter Robinson's Inspector Banks novels. Like Lewis, it is a grounded, character-led procedural built around a decent man doing a difficult job, with each story told over more than one episode.

A Touch of Frost

A Touch of Frost

1992–2010 · ITV · BritBox

David Jason plays DI Jack Frost, a rumpled and rule-bending detective who cares far more about victims than paperwork or promotion. It ran for close to two decades of feature-length cases, and like Lewis it grounds every mystery in a working detective's sense of right and wrong.

BritBox

The Inspector Lynley Mysteries

The Inspector Lynley Mysteries

2001–2007 · BBC · BritBox

Nathaniel Parker and Sharon Small play the aristocratic DI Thomas Lynley and his working-class sergeant, Barbara Havers, in this adaptation of Elizabeth George's novels. The pair's differences in background and temperament drive the show in the same way Lewis's plain-spoken style plays against Hathaway's learning.

Dalgliesh

Dalgliesh

2021–present · Channel 5 / Acorn TV · Acorn TV

Bertie Carvel plays P.D. James's reserved poet-detective Adam Dalgliesh, investigating murders in 1970s England across stories told over two episodes each. Its literary, slow-burning mysteries suit viewers who enjoyed the scholarly Oxford backdrop and Hathaway's quiet intellect.

Acorn TV

Vienna Blood

Vienna Blood

Since 2019 · BBC / ORF · PBS Masterpiece

A young Viennese doctor studying the new science of psychoanalysis teams up with a Viennese police detective to solve murders in the early 1900s. Based on Frank Tallis's novels, it pairs an intellectual outsider with a practical copper, much like Hathaway and Lewis.

PBS Masterpiece

Van der Valk

Van der Valk

2020–present · ITV · PBS Masterpiece

Marc Warren plays an Amsterdam police commissaris who relies on sharp observation and street smarts to solve feature-length cases around the city. The canals and old streets play a similar role to the Oxford colleges in Lewis, giving each mystery an elegant, distinctive setting.

Karen Pirie

Karen Pirie

2022–present · ITV · BritBox

Lauren Lyle plays a young Scottish detective who reopens long-unsolved cases in this adaptation of Val McDermid's novels. Each series tells one story across three feature-length episodes, so it suits viewers who like Lewis's unhurried, carefully plotted investigations.
