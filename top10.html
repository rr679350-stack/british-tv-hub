Fan Favourite

Inspector Morse

1987–2000  ·  ITV  ·  BritBox

The show that started it all for so many of us. John Thaw's Morse — Oxford don, opera lover, real ale enthusiast, crossword obsessive — remains the most fully realised detective in British TV history. Set against the dreaming spires of Oxford, every feature-length episode is a masterclass in character, atmosphere, and plotting.

Why fans love it "I've watched every episode three times and I still notice something new. There's nothing else like it." — From our Facebook community

Happy Valley

2014–2023  ·  BBC  ·  BritBox

Sarah Lancashire delivers one of the greatest performances in British TV history as Sergeant Catherine Cawood — a Yorkshire police officer holding her fractured family together while facing down a psychopath. Raw, powerful, deeply human, and utterly gripping from first episode to last. The final series left fans genuinely bereft when it ended.

Why fans love it "I ugly-cried at the finale. Best thing I've ever watched on television, British or otherwise." — From our Facebook community

Fan Favourite

DCI Banks

2010–2016  ·  ITV  ·  BritBox

Stephen Tompkinson is superb as Detective Chief Inspector Alan Banks, a complex, brooding detective solving dark crimes across the Yorkshire Dales. Based on Peter Robinson's bestselling novels, this is crime drama with real depth — atmospheric, intelligent, and compulsively watchable. A community favourite that never got the audience it deserved.

Why fans love it "I can't believe more people haven't seen this. It's one of the best kept secrets in British crime drama." — From our Facebook community

Fan Favourite

Line of Duty

2012–2021  ·  BBC  ·  BritBox

AC-12, an anti-corruption police unit, investigates bent coppers from within the force. The most twisty, compulsive crime series ever made in Britain — each series ratchets up the tension, and the question of who "H" is kept the entire country debating for years. The interrogation scenes alone are worth the subscription.

Why fans love it "I watched all six series in two weeks. My family barely saw me. No regrets." — From our Facebook community
Thriller Complex Unmissable BritBox
Fan Favourite

Vera

2011–present  ·  ITV  ·  BritBox

Brenda Blethyn's rumpled, brilliant DCI Vera Stanhope solving murders across the wild Northumberland coast. Warm, clever, and deeply comforting — the most consistently beloved long-running detective series in British TV. Thirteen series and still essential. The show our community recommends most to newcomers.

Why fans love it "Vera is my comfort show. I put it on when I need cheering up and it never fails." — From our Facebook community

Unforgotten

2015–present  ·  ITV  ·  PBS Passport

Long-buried cold cases surface to devastating effect on present-day lives. Quiet, slow-burning, and emotionally devastating — Nicola Walker's performance as DCI Cassie Stuart is one of the finest in British crime drama. Have tissues ready. Our community consistently describes this as the show that surprised them most.

Why fans love it "I started watching expecting a standard crime show. By episode three I was completely devastated. Nothing prepares you for it." — From our Facebook community

Shetland

2013–present  ·  BBC  ·  BritBox

Detective Jimmy Perez polices the remote Shetland Islands where the landscape is as dramatic as the crimes. Tight plotting, compelling characters, and scenery so spectacular it makes the show worth watching on its own. The location is unlike anything else in British crime drama — elemental, beautiful, and isolating.

Why fans love it "I booked a trip to Shetland after watching series one. The islands are even more beautiful in person." — From our Facebook community

Agatha Christie's Poirot

1989–2013  ·  ITV  ·  Acorn TV

David Suchet spent 25 years perfecting Hercule Poirot — and the result is one of the most remarkable achievements in British television. Every Agatha Christie novel adapted to perfection, culminating in a final episode that left the nation genuinely moved. The definitive Christie adaptation, full stop.

Why fans love it "David Suchet IS Poirot. I can't imagine anyone else in the role. 25 years of dedication and it shows in every single episode." — From our Facebook community

Hinterland

2013–2016  ·  S4C/BBC Wales  ·  Acorn TV

A detective with a dark past investigates crimes across the bleak Welsh countryside. Shot simultaneously in Welsh and English, Hinterland has a mythic, atmospheric quality unlike anything else in British crime drama. The show our community most often describes as their biggest discovery — the one they can't believe they hadn't heard of sooner.

Why fans love it "Someone in our group recommended this and I ignored it for months. When I finally watched it I was furious with myself for waiting so long." — From our Facebook community

The show our community turns to when they want to switch off and just enjoy themselves. Also available free on Pluto TV.
