Classic

The Bill

1983–2010 · ITV · Amazon Video

Twenty-seven years at Sun Hill police station — the longest-running British police procedural ever made. Before The Bill, TV coppers were tidy figures of authority. This gave them complicated marriages, drinking problems, and moral blind spots. Gritty, groundbreaking, and still compelling today. The early series in particular are essential viewing.

Why fans love it

"The Bill shaped what British police drama became. Everything we love about the genre today traces back to Sun Hill." — From our Facebook community
