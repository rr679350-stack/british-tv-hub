What Is a British Police Procedural?

A British police procedural follows professional detectives investigating crimes through evidence and process rather than shootouts — character and atmosphere over spectacle.

Modern Crime at Its Best

British police procedurals didn't peak in the eighties — the golden age is still happening. These are the modern shows the community won't stop talking about.
