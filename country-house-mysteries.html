What Is a Country-House Mystery?

A country-house mystery gathers a closed circle of suspects in a grand house, usually family, guests and servants, and cuts them off from the outside world until the killer is found. It's the classic setup of the golden age of detective fiction, and British television has been returning to it ever since.

Agatha Christie's Great Houses

Agatha Christie became one of the defining masters of the country-house murder. These four adaptations are the place to start.

What Readers Ask Most

What is a country-house mystery?

A murder mystery set in a grand house where a closed circle of suspects, usually family, guests and servants, is cut off until the killer is found. It's the classic setup of golden-age detective fiction, and Agatha Christie used it often.

Where can I stream British country-house mysteries in the US?

Poirot, Towards Zero, Why Didn't They Ask Evans?, Rosemary & Thyme and Father Brown are on BritBox. Agatha Christie's Marple and Midsomer Murders are on Acorn TV, and Magpie Murders and Death Comes to Pemberley are on PBS Masterpiece. See Where to Watch for links.

Was Dead Man's Folly really filmed at Agatha Christie's home?

Yes. The 2013 Poirot adaptation of Dead Man's Folly was filmed mainly at Greenway, Christie's own house in Devon.

Which country-house mystery is best for beginners?

The Body in the Library is the textbook setup: a grand house, a body nobody can explain, and Miss Marple. For something lighter, try Why Didn't They Ask Evans?

Are there cozy country-house mysteries that aren't too dark?

Rosemary & Thyme and Father Brown both keep things gentle, with grand houses and gardens and very little gore.
