The 6 Best British Mystery Shows Streaming Now

These are the shows that define the genre — intelligent, atmospheric, and endlessly rewatchable. Whether you're new to British television or a longtime devotee, every one of these belongs on your list.

Inspector Morse

The gold standard of British detective drama. John Thaw's Morse remains unmatched in literary intelligence and melancholic depth. Start here if you haven't already — then follow with Endeavour and Lewis.

Vera

Brenda Blethyn's Vera Stanhope is one of television's great detectives — unconventional, brilliant, and deeply human. Set against the wild Northumberland coast. Thirteen series and still essential.
