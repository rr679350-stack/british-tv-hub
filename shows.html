Beyond the household names lies some of the finest television ever made. These shows deserve far bigger audiences than they have.

Hidden Gem

The Detectorists

Mackenzie Crook's gentle comedy-drama about metal detectorists in rural Essex is perhaps the most quietly beautiful British show ever made. Two series of pure television treasure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About British Mystery Shows

Where can I stream these British mystery shows?

Every recommendation on this page includes streaming information for services such as BritBox and Acorn TV, plus free options like Tubi. For a complete breakdown by service, see the Where to Watch guide.

Which show should I watch first if I'm new to British television?

Inspector Morse, Vera, and Broadchurch are excellent starting points and remain among the finest detective dramas ever produced. For a guided path, try the Start Here page.

What's the difference between Inspector Morse, Endeavour, and Lewis?

They are all connected. Endeavour is the prequel, Inspector Morse is the original series, and Lewis continues the story after Morse.

Are these shows suitable for cozy mystery fans?

Absolutely. Many of the shows featured here offer charming villages, memorable detectives, and atmospheric storytelling with less emphasis on graphic violence. The Cozy Mysteries guide collects the gentlest of them.

How often is this guide updated?

Recommendations are reviewed and updated throughout the year to reflect new streaming availability and newly discovered hidden gems.
