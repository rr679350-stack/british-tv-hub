Weekend Binge Guide

No twelve-season commitment required. Six British series short enough to start on Friday night and finish before Monday — just 3 to 6 episodes each.

Jump to Sherlock Little Women Ludwig Happy Valley Blue Lights Trigger Point

Streaming availability may change — check BritBox, Acorn TV, or PBS Passport for current availability.

Sherlock

Sherlock

BBC · BritBox / PBS · 3 episodes per series

Benedict Cumberbatch and Martin Freeman reinvent Holmes and Watson for modern-day London, with each feature-length episode running closer to a movie than a TV hour. Series 1 alone — three 90-minute films — is a complete, satisfying weekend on its own, cliffhanger included.

At a Glance
Watch on BritBox → Buy the Series →

Little Women

Little Women

BBC · PBS Masterpiece · 3 episodes

This lovingly mounted adaptation of Louisa May Alcott's classic tells the entire March sisters saga — girlhood to adulthood, first love to loss — in just three episodes. Maya Hawke, Emily Watson, and Angela Lansbury make every minute count.

At a Glance
Watch on PBS → Buy the Series →

Ludwig

Ludwig

BBC · BritBox · 6 episodes

David Mitchell plays a reclusive puzzle-setter posing as his missing twin brother, a detective, to investigate his disappearance — solving crimes using the same logic he'd apply to a crossword. Six tightly plotted episodes with a real emotional core underneath the puzzles.

At a Glance
Watch on BritBox →

Happy Valley

Happy Valley

BBC · BritBox · 6 episodes per series

Sarah Lancashire's Sergeant Catherine Cawood carries each six-episode series alone if she has to. It's dense, intense television — six episodes feels like far more story than the runtime suggests, in the best way.

At a Glance
Watch on BritBox → Buy the Series →

Blue Lights

Blue Lights

BBC · BritBox · 6 episodes per series

Following rookie police officers on the streets of post-Troubles Belfast, each six-episode series builds real tension without ever losing sight of the people behind the uniforms. Winner of Best Drama Series at the 2025 BAFTA TV Awards, and short enough to finish before you've even finished the popcorn.

At a Glance
Watch on BritBox → Buy the Series →

Trigger Point

Trigger Point

ITV · BritBox · 6 episodes per series

Vicky McClure's bomb-disposal thriller packs relentless, white-knuckle tension into six episodes per series — the kind of show where nobody's safety is guaranteed, so the short runtime only ratchets up the stakes.
