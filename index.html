What's New on BritBox & Acorn TV

New seasons, US premieres, and what's streaming now — August 2026.

BritBox · Period Drama

A Woman of Substance

Brenda Blethyn leads this sweeping period drama. A Yorkshire maid's relentless rise to power — season finale streams August 5. Based on Barbara Taylor Bradford's beloved novel.

WATCH ON BRITBOX →
BritBox · Coming Soon

Time

David Tennant joins the cast for the third series of Jimmy McGovern's BAFTA-winning drama, written by McGovern (Unforgivable, Cracker, The Street) and Samuel Bailey (Shook). Set in a Young Offenders Institution, this series explores the impact of incarcerating teenagers and the effects on those who look after them. Coming later this year to BritBox.

WATCH ON BRITBOX →
Acorn TV · New Season

The Chelsea Detective

DI Max Arnold (Adrian Scarborough) and DS Layla Walsh (Vanessa Emme) return for a fourth season of murder cases set against the wealthy streets of Chelsea. New episodes stream Mondays through August 24 on Acorn TV.
