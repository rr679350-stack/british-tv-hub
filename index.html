Section 01 — Top Picks

The 6 Best British Mystery Shows Streaming Now

These are the shows that define the genre — intelligent, atmospheric, and endlessly rewatchable. Whether you're new to British television or a longtime devotee, every one of these belongs on your list.

No. 01 — All-Time Classic

Inspector Morse

The gold standard of British detective drama. John Thaw's Morse remains unmatched in literary intelligence and melancholic depth. Start here if you haven't already — then follow with Endeavour and Morse.

OxfordDetectiveBritBox
No. 02 — Modern Classic

Vera

Brenda Blethyn's Vera Stanhope is one of television's great detectives — unconventional, brilliant, and deeply human. Set against the wild Northumberland coast. Thirteen series and still essential.

NorthumberlandCrimeBritBox
No. 03 — Essential Viewing

Broadchurch

The defining British crime drama of the 2010s. A small coastal town, a devastating crime, and a community torn apart. Olivia Colman and David Tennant at their absolute best.

CoastalThrillerNetflix
No. 04 — Hidden Gem

Hinterland

Shot simultaneously in Welsh and English, this brooding detective series set in rural Wales is criminally underrated. Dark, atmospheric, and unlike anything else in the genre.

WalesAtmosphericAcorn TV
No. 05 — Agatha Christie Fans

Miss Marple (1984)

Joan Hickson's definitive portrayal of Miss Marple remains the benchmark. Quietly devastating, impeccably cast, and deeply satisfying to watch over and over again.

Cosy MysteryClassicBritBox
No. 06 — Currently Airing

Happy Valley

Sarah Lancashire gives a career-defining performance as a Yorkshire police sergeant navigating crime, family, and a nemesis from her past. Three series, all extraordinary.

Section 03 — Streaming Guide

How to Watch British TV in the United States

Navigating British streaming can feel overwhelming. Here is exactly where to find the best shows, without the guesswork — from free options to essential paid subscriptions.

01

BritBox — The Essential Subscription

The largest library of classic and contemporary British TV available in the US. Inspector Morse, Vera, Doc Martin, Death in Paradise and thousands more. The first subscription any serious British TV fan should get. From $8.99/month, or free with some cable packages.

02

Acorn TV — The Mystery Lover's Choice

Specialises in British and international mysteries. Home to Hinterland, Agatha Raisin, Murdoch Mysteries, and many exclusive US premieres. An excellent complement to BritBox, especially for cosy mystery fans. From $6.99/month.

03

PBS Passport — The Underrated Choice

Masterpiece Theatre classics, Call the Midwife, and PBS exclusives. Outstanding value for fans of period drama and literary adaptations. If you already donate to your local PBS station, Passport is often included. From $5/month.

04

Netflix — The Mainstream Picks

Broadchurch, The Crown, Peaky Blinders, and newer British productions. A limited but high-quality selection — a good starting point if you already subscribe, but not a substitute for a dedicated British TV service.

05

Amazon Prime — Surprising Depth

A growing and sometimes surprising collection of British dramas, often available at no extra cost with Prime membership. Worth checking before subscribing to a dedicated service — you may already have access to more than you think.

06

Tubi — The Free Option

A surprisingly large free library of British shows supported by ads. Perfect for testing the waters before committing to a paid subscription, or for catching older series you've already seen and want to revisit.

Section 04 — Frequently Asked Questions

British TV Questions — Answered

The questions British TV fans ask most, answered honestly by a community of 4,000+ enthusiasts.

What is the best British detective show of all time?

Most fans and critics point to Inspector Morse as the gold standard — its combination of literary intelligence, superb acting from John Thaw, and Oxford atmosphere has never truly been matched. For modern shows, Broadchurch and Happy Valley regularly top audience polls. Vera is the most consistently beloved long-runner.

Is BritBox worth it for British TV fans?

Yes, for serious British TV fans, BritBox is essential. It carries the largest legal library of BBC and ITV content available in the US, including classics that simply aren't available anywhere else. At under $10/month it pays for itself within days. If you can only choose one British TV subscription, make it BritBox.

Can I watch BBC shows for free in the United States?

The BBC iPlayer is only available to UK residents. In the US, BBC content reaches you through BritBox, PBS Passport, and select titles on Amazon Prime. Some older BBC content is also available free (with ads) on Tubi. There is no legitimate free access to current BBC programming from the US.

What is the most underrated British mystery series?

Hinterland consistently tops lists of overlooked gems — it is beautifully shot in rural Wales and has a genuinely distinctive atmosphere that sets it apart from every other crime series. Other underappreciated favourites from our community include The Detectorists, Hope Street, and The Bletchley Circle.

What should I watch if I loved Downton Abbey?

If you loved Downton Abbey, try Upstairs Downstairs (the original that inspired it), The Crown, Cranford, Poldark, or Victoria. All share Downton's combination of gorgeous production values, strong ensemble casts, and sweeping period drama. All Creatures Great and Small (PBS) is a gentler but equally warm choice.

What is the difference between BritBox and Acorn TV?

BritBox focuses primarily on British content from the BBC and ITV, with a vast library of classic and contemporary shows. Acorn TV has a slightly smaller library but specialises in mysteries and carries more international British-style content (including Australian and Canadian shows). Many devoted fans subscribe to both — together they cover almost everything you'd want to watch.
