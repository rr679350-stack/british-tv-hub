Shows Like Grantchester

After 11 series and a beloved vicar-and-copper partnership, Grantchester leaves behind a particular kind of warmth — period charm, found family, and mysteries that never lose sight of the people at their centre. Here's where to find more of it, and where to stream each one in the US.

Jump to Father Brown All Creatures Call the Midwife Foyle's War Miss Scarlet Endeavour Sanditon

Streaming availability may change — check BritBox or PBS Passport for current availability.

Father Brown

Father Brown

2013–present · BBC · BritBox

Swap Grantchester's vicar for a Cotswolds priest, and Cambridgeshire for the 1950s countryside — the closest match here for a clergyman with an unshakeable moral compass and a gift for solving murders.

All Creatures Great and Small

All Creatures Great and Small

2020–present · Channel 5 · PBS Masterpiece

A young vet finds his footing in a 1930s Yorkshire Dales practice, surrounded by the same kind of found-family warmth that made Grantchester's Cambridgeshire vicarage feel like home.

PBS Masterpiece

Call the Midwife

Call the Midwife

2012–present · BBC · PBS Masterpiece

Nurse-midwives and nuns serve London's East End from the late 1950s onward — a different setting than Grantchester, but the same faith-adjacent community warmth balanced against real emotional weight.

PBS Masterpiece
At a Glance
Watch on PBS → Buy the Series →

Foyle's War

Foyle's War

2002–2015 · ITV · PBS Masterpiece

A laconic detective investigates crimes on the WWII home front in a quiet English coastal town — more serious than Grantchester, but built from the same rich period detail and moral complexity.

PBS Masterpiece
At a Glance

Miss Scarlet

Miss Scarlet

2020–present · Alibi · PBS Masterpiece

Victorian London's first female private detective solves cases alongside a Scotland Yard inspector who's also her oldest friend — the same charged, will-they-won't-they chemistry that anchored Grantchester's Sidney and Amanda.

PBS Masterpiece

Endeavour

Endeavour

2012–2023 · ITV · BritBox

The young Endeavour Morse investigates murders across 1960s Oxford — a moodier, more melancholic period mystery, but sharing Grantchester's attention to period detail and a lead quietly wrestling with his own convictions.

Sanditon

Sanditon

2019–2023 · ITV · PBS Masterpiece / BritBox

Less mystery than romance, but the same Masterpiece Sunday-night slot and the same picturesque English seaside charm — a good pick for the parts of Grantchester that were really about the relationships.
