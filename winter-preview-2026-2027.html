What Readers Ask Most

What British TV shows are coming in winter 2026–27?

Confirmed so far are Christmas specials for Ludwig, The Other Bennet Sister and Death in Paradise (BritBox), Call the Midwife: Sisters in Arms and All Creatures Great and Small (PBS), and Bookish. Most exact US dates are still to come. In the new year, All Creatures Great and Small Season 7 premieres on PBS Masterpiece on January 17, 2027, and Miss Scarlet’s final season follows later in 2027.

What British Christmas specials are coming in 2026?

Ludwig, The Other Bennet Sister, Bookish (The Crown of Destiny), Call the Midwife: Sisters in Arms, All Creatures Great and Small and Death in Paradise all have confirmed Christmas specials for 2026. In the US, Ludwig, The Other Bennet Sister and Death in Paradise are coming to BritBox, while Call the Midwife and All Creatures Great and Small are coming to PBS. Bookish’s US home is still to be confirmed, and exact US dates will be added as they’re announced.

Is there a Bookish Christmas special?

Yes. Bookish: The Crown of Destiny is a two-part Christmas special airing in the UK on U&Alibi at Christmas 2026. A US release hasn’t been announced yet.

When does All Creatures Great and Small Season 7 premiere?

Season 7 premieres in the US on Sunday, January 17, 2027, at 9/8c on PBS, with seven new episodes. It also streams on the PBS app and on PBS Masterpiece on Prime Video.

Is there a Ludwig Christmas special?

Yes. David Mitchell returns for an hour-long Christmas mystery set at a remote Christmas-themed hotel in Yorkshire, where a blackout traps the guests with a murder to solve. It’s due on BritBox in December 2026; the exact US date hasn’t been announced yet.

Is The Other Bennet Sister returning?

Yes. A three-part Christmas special brings back Ella Bruccoleri as Mary Bennet, along with Ruth Jones, Dónal Finn, Tanya Reynolds and Laurie Davidson. It’s coming to BritBox for Christmas 2026, with the US date still to be announced.

Is Miss Scarlet ending?

Yes. Season 7 is the final season, with six episodes to wrap up Eliza Scarlet’s story. It’s expected on PBS Masterpiece in 2027; PBS hasn’t announced a premiere date yet.

What is coming to Acorn TV in winter 2026–27?

Acorn TV hasn’t announced its December 2026 through February 2027 US premieres yet. This guide will add them as soon as they’re confirmed.
