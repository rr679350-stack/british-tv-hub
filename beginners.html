What Readers Ask Most

What is a good first British mystery show to watch?

Midsomer Murders, Death in Paradise and Father Brown are all easy starting points. Each episode is a complete mystery, so there is no need to remember a long storyline.

Do I need to watch British mystery shows in order?

For most shows on this list, no. Midsomer Murders, Poirot, Miss Marple and Father Brown work episode by episode. Shetland and Happy Valley tell longer stories, so those are best watched from the start of a series.

What is the difference between a cozy mystery and a crime drama?

Cozy mysteries keep violence off screen and focus on the puzzle, often in a village or small community. Crime dramas such as Happy Valley are grittier and spend more time on the emotional toll of a case.

Are these shows suitable for watching with family?

The Gentle Starts and Classic Detectives sections are generally the mildest. Happy Valley contains strong violence and mature themes, so it is best saved for adult viewing.
