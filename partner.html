About Us

A Growing Community of British TV Fans

British TV Hub is an independent guide built for and by British TV fans who love mysteries and dramas — classics and new favourites alike. We're not a media company — we're a genuine community that has grown organically because we care deeply about helping people discover the best British television.

Our community has grown entirely through word of mouth — fans recommending the site to other fans, week after week. Every week we hand-pick 5 shows for our Sunday newsletter, feature a nightly pick, and publish guides that help fans navigate what to watch and where to stream it.

Our Community

Real Fans. Real Conversations. Real Growth.

Our community has grown organically around a genuine love of British TV. These are dedicated, long-term fans who don't just browse — they binge, they discuss, and they recommend shows to friends. They trust our recommendations because we're fans ourselves.

British TV Hub fans come from across the UK, US, Canada, Australia, and beyond — united by a love of the genre regardless of where they're watching from.

  • 5,000+ Facebook followers — growing week on week
  • 600+ active Facebook group members discussing shows daily
  • Weekly Sunday Picks newsletter reaching fans every Sunday morning
  • Visitors actively searching for what to watch and where to stream it
  • A community that recommends shows — and platforms — to each other constantly
What We Do

We Drive Discovery

Every piece of content we create points fans toward the shows they'll love — and the platforms where they can watch them. We feature current programming, highlight new releases, and help fans discover shows they'd never find on their own.

  • Weekly newsletter featuring new BritBox and Acorn TV releases
  • Nightly pick spotlighting one show per night with full streaming info
  • Streaming guides helping fans decide which subscription to choose
  • Hidden gems coverage bringing attention to underseen titles
  • Active Facebook community driving word-of-mouth recommendations daily
