A New Sunday Night Ritual Begins

Tonight's the night: The Marlow Murder Club returns for Season 3, and Marble Hall Murders premieres right after it — both airing back-to-back on PBS Masterpiece, every Sunday through October 11. Here's how to get ready, plus what else is worth your weekend.

Pick No. 01 · Premieres Tonight

The Marlow Murder Club — Season 3

Samantha Bond, Jo Martin, and Cara Horgan return as the unlikeliest trio of amateur sleuths in the riverside town of Marlow. Season 3 brings three new two-part mysteries — settle in, this one runs all the way to October 11.

Watch on PBS Masterpiece →

Pick No. 02 · Premieres Tonight

Marble Hall Murders

Lesley Manville returns as book editor Susan Ryeland in the third instalment of Anthony Horowitz's mystery series, following Magpie Murders and Moonflower Murders — airing right after Marlow Murder Club on the same Sunday nights.

Watch on PBS Masterpiece →

Pick No. 03 · Catch Up First

Magpie Murders

New to the Ryeland trilogy? Start here — the first installment introduces Susan Ryeland and the Atticus Pünd novels-within-the-show conceit that makes Marble Hall Murders make sense.

Watch on PBS Masterpiece →
