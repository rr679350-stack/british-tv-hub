Winter Whodunits

The nights are long and the kettle is on, which means it is the season for a proper cozy mystery. Five murder mysteries built for a cold night in, plus one hidden gem.

Pick No. 01

Sister Boniface Mysteries

A crime-solving nun with a hidden talent for forensics and a habit of outpacing the local police cracks cases in a 1960s English village. Warm, funny and about as cozy as a whodunit gets.

Watch on BritBox →

Pick No. 02

Hetty Wainthropp Investigates

A sixty-something Lancashire housewife decides, almost on a whim, that she is going to become a private investigator, and turns out to be rather good at it. Gentle, old-fashioned and perfect company on a dark evening.

Watch on BritBox →

Pick No. 03

The Chelsea Detective

A detective who still lives on a houseboat and bikes to crime scenes solves murders among London’s wealthiest residents, with an atmosphere as moody as the Thames in winter. Understated and absorbing.

Watch on Acorn TV →

Pick No. 04

Magpie Murders

An editor investigates her own murdered author, only to realize the answer may be hidden inside his unfinished manuscript. A mystery wrapped inside another mystery, and perfect for a night when you want to properly concentrate.

Watch on PBS Masterpiece →

Pick No. 05

The Last Detective

Peter Davison plays Dangerous Davies, the most overlooked, underestimated detective on the force, who keeps solving the cases nobody else wants. Quietly funny and criminally underrated.

Watch on BritBox →

Hidden Gem

Jonathan Creek

A reclusive magic consultant who lives in a converted windmill keeps getting pulled into impossible, locked-room mysteries. Clever, atmospheric and exactly the kind of puzzle a long winter evening was made for.

Watch on Prime Video →
