Maigret & The Gold Return

October is the month the big names come back. Maigret and The Gold return to PBS Masterpiece next Sunday, Ludwig is back on BritBox this Tuesday, and Acorn TV has two lovely new arrivals, plus one hidden gem worth catching up on.

Pick No. 01

Maigret: Season 2

Benjamin Wainwright returns as a young, present-day Jules Maigret, leading Paris’s serious crime unit with quiet patience and a habit of understanding people better than they understand themselves. Six new episodes begin Sunday, October 18 on PBS Masterpiece.

Watch on PBS Masterpiece →

Pick No. 02

The Gold: Season 2

Hugh Bonneville is back as DCI Brian Boyce, and the story moves into the 1990s with a surprising discovery: only half of the famous Brink’s-Mat gold has ever been traced. The search for the rest stretches all the way to sunny Spain. Six episodes begin Sunday, October 18 at 10/9c on PBS Masterpiece.

Watch on PBS Masterpiece →

Pick No. 03

Ludwig: Season 2

David Mitchell returns as the puzzle-setter turned police consultant, still brilliant with a riddle and still delightfully hopeless at everyday life. New faces this season include Stephen Merchant and Sian Clifford. Six episodes arrive weekly on BritBox from Tuesday, October 13.

Watch on BritBox →

Pick No. 04

Signora Volpe: Season 3

Emilia Fox is back as Sylvia Fox, the former spy who swapped London for the sunlit hills of Umbria. This season finds her settling back into Italian life, with new cases among the vineyards and an old face from her past. Now streaming on Acorn TV.

Watch on Acorn TV →

Pick No. 05

This Is Not a Murder Mystery

A playful new Acorn TV Original set in the 1930s, where Salvador Dalí, René Magritte and their fellow Surrealists gather at a grand English country house and find themselves at the centre of a puzzle. Stephen Tompkinson co-stars as the detective trying to keep up. It is a charming, offbeat treat, with new episodes on Mondays from October 12.

Watch on Acorn TV →

Hidden Gem

Cooper and Fry

Robert James-Collier and Mandip Gill play a mismatched pair of detectives in the Peak District, based on Stephen Booth’s much-loved novels: a local still finding his feet and a guarded newcomer from the city. The moorland scenery is gorgeous and the leads’ easy chemistry is the real treat. New episodes are rolling out weekly on BritBox, so there is still time to catch up.

Watch on BritBox →
