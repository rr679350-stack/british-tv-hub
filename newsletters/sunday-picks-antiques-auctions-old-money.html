Antiques, Auctions & Old Money

Dusty shop shelves, rare first editions and family heirlooms with a story to tell. Five mysteries where the treasure is half the fun, plus one hidden gem to settle in with.

Pick No. 01

Art Detectives

Stephen Moyer plays art-loving DI Mick Palmer of the Heritage Crime Unit, a real police department devoted to art, antiques and cultural treasures. Every case is tied to something precious, from Old Master paintings to medieval manuscripts, and the tone is light, curious and a lovely throwback to classic British mystery.

Watch on Acorn TV →

Pick No. 02

The Madame Blanc Mysteries

Sally Lindsay stars as Jean White, an antiques dealer whose sharp eye for the real thing comes in handy after she settles in a sunny French village. Charming, funny and full of beautiful old pieces, it is about as relaxing as a mystery gets.

Watch on Acorn TV →

Pick No. 03

Bookish

Mark Gatiss plays Gabriel Book, an antiquarian bookseller in 1946 London whose remarkable memory for literary and historical detail makes him an unlikely police consultant. Warm and witty, with a shop full of first editions you will want to browse yourself.

Watch on PBS Masterpiece →

Pick No. 04

Lovejoy

Ian McShane is the charming rogue of the antiques trade, wheeling and dealing around the East Anglian countryside with an uncanny knack for spotting a genuine treasure. A true classic, and pure comfort viewing for anyone who has ever lingered at a car boot sale.

Watch on BritBox →

Pick No. 05

Hustle

A crew of stylish con artists sets its sights on the greedy and the very rich, with clever schemes that always hide one more twist. Playful and great fun, it is old money finally meeting its match.

Watch on BritBox →

Hidden Gem

The Chelsea Detective

Adrian Scarborough plays DI Max Arnold, a piano-playing detective who solves cases among the grand townhouses of wealthy Chelsea while living modestly aboard a houseboat on the Thames. Thoughtful, gentle and quietly underrated.
