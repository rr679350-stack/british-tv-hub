Ludwig Returns

Our favourite puzzle-setting detective is back on BritBox this Tuesday, October 13. To get you in the mood, here are four more brainy, unconventional sleuths, plus one hidden gem.

Pick No. 01

Ludwig: Season 2

David Mitchell is back as John “Ludwig” Taylor, and this time he no longer has to pretend to be his twin brother. The Cambridge police have brought him on properly as a Crime Scene Consultant, so he gets to be fully himself: brilliant with puzzles, still hopeless at almost everything else. Six new episodes arrive weekly from October 13.

Watch on BritBox →

Pick No. 02

Professor T

Ben Miller plays Jasper Tempest, a brilliant but eccentric Cambridge criminology professor who helps the police with their trickiest cases. Like Ludwig, it pairs a wonderfully awkward genius with detectives who are never quite sure what to make of him.

Watch on PBS Masterpiece →

Pick No. 03

McDonald & Dodds

Tala Gouveia and Jason Watkins are a driven DCI and a quiet, easily overlooked sergeant who notices absolutely everything, solving cases in beautiful Bath. Another gentle hero whose unusual way of thinking wins the day.

Watch on BritBox →

Pick No. 04

Jonathan Creek

Alan Davies plays a designer of stage illusions who keeps getting drawn into seemingly impossible puzzles, from locked rooms to vanishing guests. It shares Ludwig’s joy in a baffling mystery with a clever, perfectly logical answer.

Watch on BritBox →

Pick No. 05

Death Valley

Timothy Spall plays a retired TV detective actor in the Welsh countryside who teams up with an ambitious young detective sergeant. Warm, funny and gently self-aware, it is another story about an outsider who turns out to be very good at this.

Watch on BritBox →

Hidden Gem

Queens of Mystery

A young detective sergeant solves cases in a picture-perfect English village with a little help from her three aunts, all of them crime novelists. Whimsical, colourful and full of puzzles, it is a delightful find for Ludwig fans.

Watch on Acorn TV →
