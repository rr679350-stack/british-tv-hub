Ghost Stories by the Fire

Halloween week calls for the kind of ghost story the British do best: candlelit, atmospheric and more charming than chilling. Five picks to curl up with, plus one hidden gem. If you love this mood, our guide to the BBC’s A Ghost Story for Christmas tradition is the perfect companion.

Pick No. 01

Ghosts

Alison and Mike inherit a grand but crumbling country house, only to discover it is already full of residents: the ghosts of everyone who has ever passed away there, from a Stone Age caveman to a Georgian lady. Funny, warm and surprisingly touching, it is the friendliest haunted house you will ever visit.

Watch on Paramount+ →

Pick No. 02

Agatha Christie’s Poirot: Hallowe’en Party

If you watch only one Poirot this month, make it this one. A village Halloween party takes a mysterious turn, and Poirot teams up with his friend, the crime novelist Ariadne Oliver, to get to the bottom of it. Bobbing apples, glowing lanterns and a proper Christie puzzle.

Watch on BritBox →

Pick No. 03

Midsomer Murders: Things That Go Bump in the Night

A séance, a house said to be haunted and a village full of people who may or may not believe in ghosts. Barnaby gets to the truth as always, but this episode leans beautifully into its spooky atmosphere.

Watch on Acorn TV →

Pick No. 04

Miss Scarlet & The Duke: The Vanishing

A magician disappears in the middle of his farewell performance at a London theatre, and the owner insists a resident phantom is to blame. Eliza does not believe in ghosts for a moment, and watching her prove it is pure Victorian fun.

Watch on PBS Masterpiece →

Pick No. 05

The Living and the Dead

Colin Morgan plays Nathan Appleby, a Victorian psychologist who returns with his wife to his family’s farm in the Somerset countryside, where the villagers begin reporting strange goings on. Atmospheric and beautifully filmed, it is a real slow burn for a misty October evening.

Watch on Prime Video →

Hidden Gem

Remember Me

Michael Palin plays Tom Parfitt, an elderly Yorkshireman who moves into a care home, and Jodie Comer is the young carer who becomes caught up in the mystery that seems to follow him. A three part ghost story that slipped under many radars, it is quietly haunting and wonderfully acted.

Watch on PBS Masterpiece →
