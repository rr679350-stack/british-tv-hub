Best British Female Detectives

From Jane Tennison fighting her own murder squad to Vera Stanhope working a case in a battered mac, British television has a long, rich history of female detectives who lead rather than assist. These are eleven of the best, and where to stream each show in the US.

Why These Detectives Endure

From Jane Tennison in 1991 to Karen Pirie today, British television has never been short of women leading the investigation rather than following it. Some work within the system, some solve crimes from a village tea shop, but all of them prove the detective genre was never really just one kind of story.

What Readers Ask Most

Who are the most famous British female detectives on TV?

Vera Stanhope, Jane Tennison, and Miss Marple are among the most iconic. More recent additions include Karen Pirie, Annika Strandhed, and Cassie Stuart from Unforgotten.

Which of these detective shows are on BritBox?

Vera, Prime Suspect, Karen Pirie, Miss Marple, Blue Lights, and Sister Boniface Mysteries are all available on BritBox in the US. See the BritBox Guide for the full catalogue.

Which of these detective shows are on Acorn TV?

Whitstable Pearl and Agatha Raisin are both available on Acorn TV in the US.

Which of these detective shows are on PBS Masterpiece?

Unforgotten, Annika, and Miss Scarlet have all aired as part of PBS Masterpiece, and are available with PBS Passport.

Are any of these shows based on books?

Several are. Vera and Miss Marple are based on Ann Cleeves and Agatha Christie respectively, Karen Pirie is based on Val McDermid's novels, Whitstable Pearl is based on Julie Wassmer's books, and Agatha Raisin is based on the M.C. Beaton series.

What should I watch after Vera?

Cassie Stuart in Unforgotten shares Vera's character-driven, slow-burn approach. For something a bit lighter, Sister Boniface Mysteries or Agatha Raisin both keep the cozy village-mystery feel.
