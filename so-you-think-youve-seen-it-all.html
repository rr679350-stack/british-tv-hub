So You Think You've Seen It All?

Everyone's seen Vera and Broadchurch. This is for what comes after — four rounds of British mystery, each one a little further off the beaten path than the last.

Jump to Beyond the Usual Favorites How About These? Surely Not All of These All Right, You Win

Eleven shows across three levels, plus a final challenge. Some links may be affiliate links — see disclosure below.

Level 1 — Beyond the Usual Favorites

Good shows that aren't obscure, but aren't the same titles everyone recommends.

The Long Call

2021 · ITV · BritBox

An Ann Cleeves adaptation with Ben Aldridge as DI Matthew Venn — it only ran four episodes, which makes it surprisingly easy to have missed.

The Chelsea Detective

2022–present · Acorn TV · Acorn TV

London mysteries with an unusual detective and a lovely setting — Adrian Scarborough's DI Max Arnold works Chelsea's glossiest streets from a modest houseboat.

Dalgliesh

2021–present · Channel 5 · Acorn TV

A P.D. James adaptation starring Bertie Carvel as the enigmatic detective-poet Adam Dalgliesh, set in the early 1980s and adapted with real restraint.

Mrs Sidhu Investigates

2023 · Acorn TV · Acorn TV

Meera Syal as a Berkshire caterer with a knack for solving crimes — four feature-length episodes of warm, funny, gently cozy mystery that never got a second series.

Level 2 — How About These?

Now we're getting beyond the most familiar recommendations.

The Tower

2021–2024 · ITV · BritBox

A Metropolitan Police procedural based on Kate London's novels, written by an actual former Met murder-squad detective. Strong storytelling, nowhere near the recognition of Line of Duty.

3 series, completeBritBox
At a Glance
Watch on BritBox →

Manhunt

2019–2021 · ITV · Acorn TV

Martin Clunes in a restrained, true-crime police procedural — a real change of pace from Doc Martin, as DCI Colin Sutton hunts a serial killer using nothing but painstaking, unglamorous police work.

2 seasons, completeAcorn TV
At a Glance
Watch on Acorn TV →

Hidden

2018–2021 · BBC/S4C · Acorn TV

Welsh crime drama set against the landscape of North Wales — from the same team behind Hinterland, with Sian Reese-Williams as a detective drawn into disappearances in Snowdonia.

3 series, completeAcorn TV
At a Glance
Watch on Acorn TV →

The Mallorca Files

2019–present · BBC One · BritBox

A British-German detective pairing solving cases on the sun-drenched island of Mallorca — Elen Rhys and Julian Looman make an unlikely, very watchable odd couple.

Level 3 — Surely Not All of These

This is where it gets genuinely difficult.

Rebus

2000–2007 · ITV · BritBox

Ian Rankin's Edinburgh detective, played first by John Hannah and then by Ken Stott across four series — long before the newer Rebus adaptation, and easy to have missed entirely.

4 series, completeBritBox
At a Glance
Watch on BritBox →

The Commander

2003–2008 · ITV · BritBox

Amanda Burton as Metropolitan Police Commander Clare Blake, from Prime Suspect creator Lynda La Plante — five series of a tough, ambitious lead who rarely gets mentioned in the same breath as Jane Tennison.

5 series, completeBritBox
At a Glance
Watch on BritBox →

Case Sensitive

2011–2012 · ITV · BritBox

Olivia Williams and Darren Boyd in two feature-length adaptations of Sophie Hannah's psychological crime novels — a short, sharp run that came and went with little fanfare.
