What Readers Ask Most

What are the best British mystery shows to watch in 2026?

Shetland, Line of Duty, and Blue Lights are among the biggest returning crime dramas in 2026, while The Marlow Murder Club, Ludwig, and Marble Hall Murders bring cozier, character-driven mysteries with new seasons this year.

Where can I stream British mystery shows in the US?

Most modern crime dramas stream on BritBox. Cozy village mysteries and several Acorn Originals live on Acorn TV. Literary adaptations including Unforgotten, Maigret, and Marble Hall Murders are on PBS Masterpiece. See the Where to Stream section above for the full breakdown.

What British mystery show should I start with?

Shetland is one of the most accessible starting points — atmospheric and character-driven without being too dark. For something cozier, The Marlow Murder Club or Midsomer Murders are gentle, comforting entry points.

Which of these shows are getting new seasons soonest?

Ludwig Season 2 arrives October 13, 2026, Maigret Season 2 premieres October 18, 2026, and The Marlow Murder Club Season 3 premieres September 6, 2026 alongside Marble Hall Murders — both on PBS Masterpiece.

Is this list only currently-airing shows?

This guide focuses on British mystery and detective series that are currently airing, confirmed for a new season, or actively renewed — rather than beloved classics that have already concluded, like Poirot or Inspector Morse.
