Best British Mysteries on Acorn TV

Acorn TV specializes in British and international mysteries, and its catalogue runs deeper than the monthly new-arrivals list. From easy entry points to under-the-radar gems, these are the standout British mysteries and dramas worth your subscription, organized by mood.

What Makes Acorn TV Worth It

Acorn TV's library is smaller than BritBox's, but it's more tightly focused on mystery and crime drama — plus a run of genuine Acorn TV Originals you won't find anywhere else. This guide sorts the best of it by what kind of mystery you're after, not just what's newest.

What Readers Ask Most

What are the best British mysteries on Acorn TV?

Doc Martin and Whitstable Pearl are great starting points. For something more literary, Dalgliesh and Foyle's War are excellent. Art Detectives, Signora Volpe, and The Chelsea Detective are recent Acorn TV Originals worth seeking out.

What's the difference between this guide and the monthly Acorn TV guide?

This page is an evergreen, curated list of Acorn TV's best British mysteries across the whole catalogue. The monthly Acorn TV Guide covers what's new arriving each month.

Are any of these shows complete, with no more seasons coming?

Yes. Foyle's War, Manhunt, Bloodlands, Mrs Sidhu Investigates, Pie in the Sky, Kingdom, and The Detectorists have all concluded, so you can watch the whole story without waiting for renewals.

Which of these are Acorn TV Originals?

Mrs Sidhu Investigates, Signora Volpe, The Chelsea Detective, Dalgliesh, and Art Detectives were all made specifically for Acorn TV.
