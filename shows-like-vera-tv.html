Shows Like Vera

Vera's mix of atmosphere, character, and a detective who works on instinct as much as evidence has no shortage of fans. Here are the British crime dramas worth watching after Vera, and where to stream each one in the US.

Jump to Shetland Blue Lights Dalgliesh Whitstable Pearl Karen Pirie Unforgotten Grace DCI Banks Scott & Bailey

Streaming availability may change — check BritBox, Acorn TV, PBS Passport, or Prime Video for current availability.

Shetland

Shetland

2013–present · BBC · BritBox

Ann Cleeves' other bestselling detective series, the closest match to Vera in tone — slow-burn, landscape-driven mysteries set against the bleak, beautiful Shetland Islands.
