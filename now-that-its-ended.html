Now That It's Ended

Some shows get a proper finale. Others just… stop, with no warning and no goodbye. If one of your favourites recently went quiet, here's what happened — and where to go next.

Four recently-ended favourites · Some links may be affiliate links — see disclosure below
Jump to Bloodlands Annika Whitstable Pearl McDonald & Dodds

Every series here is complete and still streaming — nothing has been pulled, there's just no more coming. Where to stream each one is noted below.

Bloodlands

Bloodlands

2021–2022 · BBC · Acorn TV

James Nesbitt's tense Belfast thriller wrapped its second series with Tom Brannick's secret finally catching up with him — and it turned out to be the show's real ending. A third series was hoped for, but the BBC confirmed there are no plans to bring it back.

Annika

Annika

2021–2023 · UKTV/Alibi · PBS Masterpiece

Nicola Walker's witty, fourth-wall-breaking marine detective was one of the more purely charming leads in recent British TV — sharp, funny, and quietly moving. UKTV confirmed there are no plans for a third series, so two seasons is the whole story.

Whitstable Pearl

Whitstable Pearl

2021–2024 · Acorn TV · Acorn TV

Kerry Godliman's seaside restaurant-owner-turned-detective ran for three warm, gentle series before Acorn TV quietly moved on from the show, leaving Pearl and DCI Mike McGuire's will-they-won't-they right where it stood.

McDonald & Dodds

McDonald & Dodds

2020–2024 · ITV · BritBox

Four series of an odd-couple pairing that really worked — the streetwise DCI Lauren McDonald and the endearingly overlooked DS Dodds, solving crimes around Bath. ITV confirmed there's no series five planned, so series four is where it ends.
