Last Days of Summer

That bittersweet stretch before autumn creeps in — five shows to savor while the light's still golden, plus one hidden gem worth tracking down.

Pick No. 01

Doc Martin

A grumpy former surgeon trades London for a tiny Cornish village, and somehow the scenery steals almost every scene. Warm, funny, and soaked in that late-summer coastal light.

Watch on BritBox →

Pick No. 02

The Durrells

A family swaps grey England for sun-drenched Corfu, and the result is pure golden nostalgia — messy, warm, and full of that "just one more summer" feeling.

Watch on PBS Masterpiece →

Pick No. 03

Death in Paradise

Easy island charm and a detective who never quite settles into paradise. Perfect for stretching that summer feeling a little further, wherever you're watching from.

Watch on BritBox →

Pick No. 04

Grantchester

Long warm evenings, village fêtes, and a vicar who can't resist getting pulled into a mystery. Cambridgeshire in the 1950s has rarely looked so inviting.

Watch on PBS Masterpiece →

Pick No. 05

The Detectorists

Quiet, wistful, and gently funny — two friends wander the English countryside with metal detectors, chasing treasure and turning up something closer to peace.

Watch on Acorn TV →

Hidden Gem

The Café

An easy-to-miss little sitcom set in a seafront café in Weston-super-Mare — only two series, but full of the same warm, small-town charm that makes a good seaside escape.

Watch on BritBox →
