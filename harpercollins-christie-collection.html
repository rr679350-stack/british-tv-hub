The HarperCollins Agatha Christie Collection

Ten HarperCollins titles worth having on your shelf — from a brand-new Miss Marple continuation novel to the bestselling mystery of all time. Whether you're building out your collection or looking for where to start, here's where to begin.

A Shelf Worth Building

Agatha Christie remains the bestselling novelist of all time, and HarperCollins keeps her catalogue — new continuation novels included — in print and in good company. This isn't a ranking so much as a starting shelf: a mix of classics, a true standalone, an anthology, an audiobook, and the newest addition to the Marple universe.

The Collection

Ten titles, in no particular order — pick whichever one calls to you first.

01
New for 2026 · A Miss Marple Mystery

Murder at the Grand Alpine Hotel

By Lucy Foley · Created by Agatha Christie

The first-ever continuation novel to bring Miss Marple back, written by Sunday Times bestseller Lucy Foley and snowbound in the Alps with a body in the cable car.

What Readers Ask Most

Where should I start with Agatha Christie?

And Then There Were None is the classic starting point — it's Christie's bestselling novel and a self-contained standalone, so there's no series order to worry about. Why Didn't They Ask Evans? is another good standalone pick.

What is Murder at the Grand Alpine Hotel?

It's a brand-new 2026 continuation novel written by Lucy Foley and authorized by the Christie estate — the first continuation novel to feature Miss Marple. It's a standalone mystery, not part of the original Marple series.

Is Marple: Expert on Wickedness available now?

It's currently available for pre-order and is scheduled to release on February 2, 2027. It's a biography of the character Miss Marple by Dr Mark Aldridge, not a Christie novel.
