What Readers Ask Most

What's the best decade for British mystery TV?

There's no single answer, but the 2010s are often cited as a high point, producing Line of Duty, Happy Valley, Broadchurch, Vera, and Shetland within a few years of each other. The 1980s gave the genre its first true icons in Poirot, Morse, and Miss Marple.

What's the oldest British mystery show still worth watching?

The Rivals of Sherlock Holmes (1971, BritBox) and Van der Valk (1972, PBS Masterpiece) are among the oldest widely available British mystery and detective series still streaming in the US.

Where can I stream classic British mystery shows from the 1980s and 1990s in the US?

Most classics from these decades, including Inspector Morse, Poirot, Miss Marple, and Prime Suspect, stream on BritBox. Some, like Midsomer Murders, are on Acorn TV instead.

What British mysteries are airing or renewed right now?

As of late 2026, Blue Lights, Ludwig, Sherwood, and Dalgliesh all have current or recently renewed seasons on BritBox or Acorn TV in the US. The Gold concluded its two-season run on PBS Masterpiece in 2026.
