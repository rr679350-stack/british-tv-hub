From Miss Marple to The Marlow Murder Club

Behind every cozy village mystery on television is usually a novel — and often a whole shelf of them. These are the authors worth reading if you love the gentler side of British crime, from the genre's founder to the writers keeping it going today.

What Readers Ask Most

Who are some notable cozy mystery authors?

Agatha Christie, M.C. Beaton, Ann Cleeves, and Richard Osman are among the most widely read, with Rhys Bowen, G.M. Malliet, Robert Thorogood, and Alexander McCall Smith close behind for readers who've worked through the classics.

Which of these authors have TV adaptations?

Agatha Christie, Ann Cleeves, M.C. Beaton, and Robert Thorogood have all had their books adapted for television.

What's a good starting point for someone new to cozy mystery novels?

Agatha Christie's Miss Marple novels are the genre's foundation, and Richard Osman's Thursday Murder Club is the most talked-about modern entry point.

Is Robert Thorogood the same person who created Death in Paradise?

Yes. Thorogood created Death in Paradise for the BBC before writing The Marlow Murder Club novels, which were later adapted into their own TV series.

Are M.C. Beaton's books still being written?

M.C. Beaton (Marion Chesney) died in 2019, but both the Agatha Raisin and Hamish Macbeth series have continued under the M.C. Beaton name, written by R.W. Green.
