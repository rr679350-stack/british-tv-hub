Privacy Policy
Last Updated: July 2026

Who We Are

British TV Hub (britishtvhub.com) is an independent fan site dedicated to British mystery and drama television, run by a single person for a community of fellow fans. The Cozy Mystery Guide chat assistant on this site is powered by Chatbase. Our Etsy shop operates as CozyTVCompanion at cozytvcompanion.etsy.com.

For any privacy questions, contact us at info@britishtvhub.com.

Information We Collect

We collect very little personal information. Specifically:

  • Newsletter signups: If you subscribe to the Sunday Picks newsletter, we collect your email address via Mailchimp. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Chat conversations: When you use The Cozy Mystery Guide chat assistant, your messages may be stored by Chatbase to improve response quality. We do not collect your name or email through the chat unless you voluntarily provide it.
  • General browsing: Like most websites, our hosting provider (Vercel) may log basic information such as your IP address and browser type for security and performance purposes.

We do not run advertising, sell your data, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • Email addresses are used only to send the Sunday Picks newsletter you signed up for.
  • Chat conversation data is used only to improve The Cozy Mystery Guide responses.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers.

Affiliate Links & Advertising

British TV Hub uses affiliate links. This means we may earn a small commission if you make a purchase through links on our site, at no extra cost to you. We only recommend products and services we genuinely believe in.

Current affiliate partnerships include:

  • Amazon Associates (britishtvhub-20)
  • NordVPN
  • Bookshop.org (via ShareASale)

Affiliate links are disclosed where they appear. British TV Hub is not paid to recommend specific shows or streaming services — all recommendations reflect genuine opinion.

Third Party Services

Our site uses the following third party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Chatbase — powers The Cozy Mystery Guide chat assistant
  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Mailchimp — Sunday Picks newsletter delivery
  • Facebook — our community page and Cozy Corner group
  • Etsy — CozyTVCompanion shop (governed by Etsy's own privacy policy)
  • Squarespace — DNS management

Cookies

Our site may use basic cookies for functionality and performance. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising or retargeting purposes. The Chatbase chat widget may set its own cookies — please refer to Chatbase's privacy policy for details.

Your Rights (UK & EU Visitors)

If you are located in the United Kingdom or European Union, you have rights under GDPR including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To make a request, please email info@britishtvhub.com and we will respond within 30 days.

To unsubscribe from the Sunday Picks newsletter, use the unsubscribe link in any email or contact us directly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date at the top. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For any privacy questions, concerns, or requests please email us at info@britishtvhub.com. We're a small independent site and will always do our best to respond promptly.
